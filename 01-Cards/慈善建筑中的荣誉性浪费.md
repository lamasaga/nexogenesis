## 模式描述

　　即便是 ostensibly 以改善人类生活为目的的公益遗赠，在执行中也常被攀比文化准则 redirect：资金被用于建造昂贵、装饰繁复、具有城堡/哥特式军事风格的建筑，窗户与内部布局优先向外展示金钱价值，而非提升受助者便利与舒适，形成公益目标与荣誉性浪费之间的置换。

## 典型实例

- foundling asylum、retreat for invalids 等慈善机构外墙铺设昂贵石材、覆盖怪诞细节、城墙垛口与塔楼。
- 窗户 placement 旨在让外部旁观者留下金钱卓越印象，而非内部受益者便利。
- 所有相关方默认 honorific 细节服务于"更高或精神需求"。

## 反例与失效条件

- 原文未给出此类 redirect 的资金比例。
- 仅描述为一种普遍到"不足为奇"的现象，未讨论是否存在有效监督机制避免此情况。

## 原文摘录

> "An appreciable share of the funds is spent in the construction of an edifice faced with some aesthetically objectionable but expensive stone, covered with grotesque and incongruous details, and designed, in its battlemented walls and turrets and its massive portals and strategic approaches, to suggest certain barbaric methods of warfare."
> "The windows, for instance... are placed with a view to impress their pecuniary excellence upon the chance beholder from the outside, rather than with a view to effectiveness for their ostensible end in the convenience or comfort of the beneficiaries within."
> "Where these honorific details occur, in such cases, they commonly masquerade under designations that belong in the field of the aesthetic, ethical or economic interest."
