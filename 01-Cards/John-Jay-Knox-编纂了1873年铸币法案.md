### 定义

John Jay Knox（1828-1892）是美国货币监理署官员，1867 年任副署长，后任署长至 1884 年。他于 1870 年向国会提交铸币法律法典化报告，将 80 多年来 60 多项铸币法律整合为修订法典中约 12 页的条文，形成后来的 1873 年《铸币法案》。

### 关键属性

- **1870 年报告**： Senate Mis. Doc., No. 132, XLI. Cong., 2d Sess.，内容包括铸币法律法典化及重要修正。
- **1873 年法案**：附带法案经轻微修改后通过，即"the Coinage Act of 1873"。
- **恢复铸币支付**：1878 年 4 月，他与 Sherman 财长、Devens 司法部长赴纽约谈判，促成 Rothschild 财团通过 August Belmont 以 1.5% 溢价认购 5000 万美元 4.5% 债券，用于恢复铸币准备。
- **政治立场**：1885 年 2 月在波士顿商人俱乐部演讲中猛烈批评共和、民主两党在银币问题上只说"陈词滥调"，主张克利夫兰政府废除有利于银矿业的铸币法。

### 边界与局限

本书对 Knox 的叙述带有颂扬性；未讨论 1873 年法案中白银条款的政治争议、以及他在恢复铸币支付中的实际决策权重。

### 原文摘录

> "In 1870 he made an elaborate report to Congress (Senate Mis. Doc., No. 132, XLI. Cong., 2d Sess.), including a codification of the Mint and Coinage laws, with important amendments..."
> "The bill which accompanied the report comprised, within the compass of twelve pages of the Revised Statutes, every important provision contained in more than sixty different enactments upon the Mint and Coinage of the United States—the result of eighty years of legislation."
> "In April, 1878, he accompanied Secretary Sherman and Attorney-General Devens to New York... Upon the return... they were met by August Belmont, who had a cable dispatch from the Rothschilds, authorizing a purchase of the whole amount at a premium of one and one-half per cent."
> "The platforms of both parties in the late campaign contained nothing but platitudes upon the silver question, which should have been the burning issue."
