## 模式描述

　　现代教育中的仪式遗存——学位服、入学/毕业典礼、学术等级与特权——是有闲阶级生活方式在认知领域的延续。当学校从服务勤劳阶级的实用教育转向服务有闲/神职阶级的高等人文教育时，学术仪式与排场会同步增长；这类仪式既迎合古代象征主义与壮观效果的返祖倾向，也包含显著的炫耀性浪费要素。

## 典型实例

- 美国中西部大学：当社区财富积累、学校依赖有闲阶级选民时，学位帽、学位袍等学术仪式明显增加。
- 实用学校模仿这些仪式以符合上层学术体面标准。
- 学校从为下层阶级提供即时有用的知识，转向为神职与有闲阶级准备按 conventionally accepted scope and method 消费知识。
- 美国内战后出现向野蛮状态返祖的社会情绪浪潮，表现为对地位、拟人化信仰与保守主义的强调；学位帽与学位袍的复兴是这一返祖浪潮的遥远、深奥表达，也标志着大学在财富积累足以支撑有闲阶级要求后，已实际或志向上成为有闲阶级机构。

## 反例与失效条件

- 原文承认存在例外，特别是由典型体面且仪式化的教会创办的学校。
- 论证主要是相关性与历史演化，而非因果量化。
- 原文未提供量化指标来衡量学术仪式流行的峰值；其时间归因主要基于观察与推断。

## 原文摘录

> "The ritualistic features of the educational system of the present and of the recent past... have their place primarily in the higher, liberal, and classic institutions and grades of learning, rather than in the lower, technological, or practical grades."
> "So soon as wealth begins appreciably to accumulate in the community, and so soon as a given school begins to lean on a leisure-class constituency, there comes also a perceptibly increased insistence on scholastic ritual and on conformity to the ancient forms."
> "Cap and gown have been adopted as learned insignia by many colleges of this section within the last few years... it at the same time fits into the leisure-class scheme of life as involving a notable element of conspicuous waste."
> "The wave of reversion seems to have received its initial impulse in the psychologically disintegrating effects of the Civil War."
> "The learned ritual and paraphernalia here spoken of are a still remoter and more recondite expression of the barbarian animistic sense; and these, therefore, gained vogue and elaboration more slowly and reached their most effective development at a still later date."
> "The adoption of the cap and gown is one of the striking atavistic features of modern college life, and at the same time it marks the fact that these colleges have definitely become leisure-class establishments, either in actual achievement or in aspiration."
