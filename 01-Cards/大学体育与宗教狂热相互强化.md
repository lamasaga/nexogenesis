## 模式描述

　　大学中的运动员往往同时是虔诚的宗教实践者；宗教组织也利用体育比赛作为皈依手段与维持信徒虔诚态度的工具，因为二者调动的是同一范围的心理倾向。在美国和英国学校中，大学体育还已成为古典学问在休闲教育中的竞争者：体育成功不仅意味着浪费时间，还意味着浪费金钱，并体现某些高度非工业化的返祖性格特质，因此作为有闲阶级学问的凭证比古典学问更具优势。

## 典型实例

- 大学运动员常投身宗教宣传，倾向于传播 more anthropomorphic cults。
- YMCA、Christian Endeavor 等宗教团体投入精力推动体育竞赛。
- 体育被认为具有某种 efficacy as a means of grace。
- 古典学问直到近年在欧洲大陆几乎没有对手；自大学体育被认可为正式学术成就领域以来，它便成为英美学校中有闲阶级教育的新竞争者。
- 体育相比古典学问的明显优势在于：成功运动员预设了时间浪费、金钱浪费，以及特定的高度非工业化 archaic 性格气质；在德国大学中，类似功能部分由技巧化的酗酒与敷衍的决斗承担。

## 反例与失效条件

- 该观察基于作者时代的美国大学文化，未必适用于所有国家或所有时期的大学。
- 不能概括所有运动员或所有宗教组织。
- 原文将体育称为"if athletics may be freely classed as learning"，带有讽刺意味；未讨论体育对认知能力的实际影响。

## 原文摘录

> "The desirable athletic material afforded by any student body in this country is at the same time predominantly religious."
> "These lay religious bodies commonly devote some appreciable portion of their energies to the furtherance of athletic contests and similar games of chance and skill."
> "The games which give exercise to the animistic sense and to the emulative propensity help to form and to conserve that habit of mind to which the more esoteric cults are congenial."
> "Since college athletics have won their way into a recognized standing as an accredited field of scholarly accomplishment, this latter branch of learning--if athletics may be freely classed as learning--has become a rival of the classics for the primacy in leisure-class education in American and English schools."
> "Athletics have an obvious advantage over the classics for the purpose of leisure-class learning, since success as an athlete presumes, not only waste of time, but also waste of money, as well as the possession of certain highly unindustrial archaic traits of character and temperament."
