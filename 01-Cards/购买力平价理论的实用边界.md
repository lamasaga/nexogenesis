### 一句话主张

　　购买力平价在实际应用中面临运输成本、关税、非贸易品、指数构造等多重困难；它是指示两国货币相对购买力变化的重要分析工具，但不能直接作为汇率的应然目标或长期预测器。

### 依据

　　实际计算通常以 1913 年为基准，假设当时的内外购买力差异可代表当前调整因素。若把指数局限于贸易品，理论退化为同义反复（套利确保价格不会长期偏离）。若扩展至一般物价指数，又需假设非贸易品价格与贸易品同比例变动，而战争等冲击使该假设不可靠。Cassel 承认其计算严格依赖“各国价格上涨同等地影响所有商品”这一前提。

### 已知限制

　　原文未提出改进的实证方法；其批判主要是方法论层面的。不同物价指数会强化或弱化该结论。

### 原文摘录

> In practical applications of the doctrine there are, however, two further difficulties... both of them arising out of the words allowance being made for transport charges and import and export taxes.
> The second difficulty--the treatment of purchasing power over articles which do not enter into international trade--is still more serious.
> If we restrict ourselves to articles entering into international trade and make exact allowance for transport and tariff costs, we should find that the theory is always in accordance with the facts, with perhaps a short time-lag.
> Purchasing power parity, thus defined, tells us an important fact about the relative changes in the purchasing power of money... but it does not necessarily settle what the equilibrium exchange rate... ought to be.
