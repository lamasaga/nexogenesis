### 定义

William E. Du Bois（1810-1884）是美国铸币局助理化验师（Assistant Assayer），1838 年在费城铸币局创办钱币柜（Cabinet of Coins），并长期负责其扩充与管理。

### 关键属性

- **钱币柜创办**：1838 年开始筹建，每年从国会获得小额拨款，收藏工作完全交给 Du Bois。
- **收藏理念**：1846 年出版描述性著作《Pledges of History, etc.》，认为硬币应因其历史教导或艺术价值而被珍视， discourage "仅仅因为稀有而占有"的狂热。
- **合著与著作**：与 Jacob R. Eckfeldt 合著 1842 年《Manual of the Gold and Silver Coins of all nations, struck within the past century》，1850-1851 年出补编；为美国哲学学会撰写《The Natural Dissemination of Gold》；1869 年在《Bankers' Magazine》发表《Propositions for a Revised System of Weights, and a Restoration of Silver Currency》。
- **化验改革**：在 1837 年化验方法从 carats/grains 改为千分制、以及湿法化验替代旧式银化验法的过程中直接监督实施。

### 边界与局限

原文未说明钱币柜的后续规模、Du Bois 个人收藏与官方收藏的界限、以及 1869 年银币恢复文章的具体论点。

### 原文摘录

> "He instituted the Cabinet of coins which now adorns the Mint. This was commenced in 1838. A small annual appropriation was procured from Congress for this purpose, and the work of collection committed entirely to Mr. Du Bois."
> "The title thus selected intimated his opinion as to the real value of such collections. He thought that a coin should be prized for its historical teaching, or artistic merit, and discouraged the rage to possess a piece simply because of its rarity."
> "In 1837, on revision of the Mint laws and standards brought about by Dr. Robert M. Patterson, then Director, a reform was effected in the method of reporting assays... About this time, also, the older plan of assaying silver was abandoned, the humid assay being substituted..."
