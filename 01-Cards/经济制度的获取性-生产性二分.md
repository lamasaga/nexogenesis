## 核心思想

　　经济制度可依据功能分为两类：服务于 invidious 利益的获取性（pecuniary/business）制度，与服务非攀比性利益的生产性（industrial）制度。leisure class 主要与前一类相关，其利益在于财产的保有与转移。

## 关键组件

- **获取性制度**：与所有权、金融操作、商业惯例相关，服务于 invidious 经济兴趣。
- **生产性制度**：与 workmanship、机械生产过程相关，服务于 non-invidious 经济兴趣。
- **leisure class 的功能**：其经济关系是获取性的、寄生性的，利益在于 divert and retain substance。
- **工业制度被忽视**：因其不直接涉及统治阶级，很少成为立法或 deliberate convention 的主题。

## 结构关系/因果链条

社会存在两类经济兴趣（攀比 vs 非攀比）→ 形成获取性制度与生产性制度 → leisure class 维护前者 → 工业制度在正式规则中被低估。

## 失效边界

　　该二分法高度抽象，现实中个人往往同时参与金钱与工业活动；作者也承认这不是 hard and fast distinction。

## 原文摘录

> "They are institutions of acquisition or of production... they are pecuniary or industrial institutions... they are institutions serving either the invidious or the non-invidious economic interest."
> "The relation of the leisure (that is, propertied non-industrial) class to the economic process is a pecuniary relation--a relation of acquisition, not of production; of exploitation, not of serviceability."
> "Their office is of a parasitic character, and their interest is to divert what substance they may to their own use, and to retain whatever is under their hand."
