### 核心表达

　　专业人士与业余者的区别在于看待事物的方式。专业人士专注于做正确的事，而非直接想着赚钱；他知道只要其他事情处理得当，利润自会到来。

### 依据与细节

　　作者说，一个人在一件事上花费多年会形成与初学者截然不同的习惯性态度。"The difference distinguishes the professional from the amateur." 公众以业余爱好者态度对待自己的努力，自我过度介入，思考不深入。专业人士关注做正确的事，知道利润会随之而来。"A trader gets to play the game as the professional billiard player does--that is, he looks far ahead instead of considering the particular shot before him." 这是为位置而打的直觉。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未说明如何定义"正确的事"；其观点强调过程重于结果。

### 原文摘录

> A man can’t spend years at one thing and not acquire a habitual attitude towards it quite unlike that of the average beginner. The difference distinguishes the professional from the amateur.
> The professional concerns himself with doing the right thing rather than with making money, knowing that the profit takes care of itself if the other things are attended to.
> A trader gets to play the game as the professional billiard player does--that is, he looks far ahead instead of considering the particular shot before him.
