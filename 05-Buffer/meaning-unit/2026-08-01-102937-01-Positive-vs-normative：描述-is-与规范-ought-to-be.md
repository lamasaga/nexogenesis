**Positive statement** 断言世界 **is** 如何；**normative statement** 断言世界 **ought to be** 如何。Positive 可凭 facts 与 scientific method 评判；normative 须 **value judgments**。经济学家做 normative statements 时 acting more as **policy advisers** than as **scientists**——与 2-2a Prisha/Noah 例证一脉相承。
