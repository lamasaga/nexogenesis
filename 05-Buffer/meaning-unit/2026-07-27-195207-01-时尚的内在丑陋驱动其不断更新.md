### 核心表达

　　浪费要求服饰明显无用，而无用性又冒犯 native taste；于是每一种时尚都急于伪装某种表面用途，但这种伪装很快变得透明可憎，迫使社会逃向同样无用的下一波时尚。因此 fashionable attire 本质丑陋且不断变化。

### 依据与细节

　　作者提出心理规律：所有人——尤其是女性——厌恶徒劳，"abhor futility, whether of effort or of expenditure"。但炫耀性浪费要求"obviously futile expenditure"，因此服装的昂贵性"is therefore intrinsically ugly"。每一轮创新都试图"avoid condemnation by showing some ostensible purpose"，但浪费规范使其目的性只能停留于"somewhat transparent pretense"。当这种无用性"forces itself so baldly upon our attention as to become unbearable"，人们就"take refuge in a new style"。新风格同样必须符合浪费与无用要求，因此"its futility presently becomes as odious as that of its predecessor"，唯一出路是再求新。作者总结："Hence the essential ugliness and the unceasing change of fashionable attire."

### 限制与边界

　　该模型假设存在稳定的"native taste"或"abiding aesthetic sense"作为评判基准，但未说明其具体内容或跨文化差异。它更多解释时尚周期而非具体风格内容。

### 原文摘录

> "The standard of reputability requires that dress should show wasteful expenditure; but all wastefulness is offensive to native taste."
> "Even in its freest flights, fashion rarely if ever gets away from a simulation of some ostensible use."
> "Hence the essential ugliness and the unceasing change of fashionable attire."
