### 核心表达

　　携带手杖这一看似琐碎的习惯，同时传递两个信号：一是表明双手未从事有用劳动，二是满足野蛮人对有形武器的舒适感。它在 leisure class、运动人士与下层罪犯中最为盛行。

### 依据与细节

　　作者以 walking-stick 为例说明 predaceous impulse 在不同阶级中的分布差异。"The classes among whom the habit most prevails... are the men of the leisure class proper, sporting men, and the lower-class delinquents"，可能还包括从事金钱职业者。普通工业劳动者与女性（除非因虚弱需要支撑）不携带手杖。手杖首先是闲暇证据："The walking-stick serves the purpose of an advertisement that the bearer's hands are employed otherwise than in useful effort"。但它同时也是武器，"meets a felt need of barbarian man on that ground"。握持如此具体、原始的攻击工具，对具有适度 ferocity 的人是"very comforting"。

### 限制与边界

　　该解释对手杖的文化象征意义做了高度还原。现代视角下手杖也可能代表时尚配饰、身份标志或身体辅助工具。

### 原文摘录

> "The walking-stick serves the purpose of an advertisement that the bearer's hands are employed otherwise than in useful effort, and it therefore has utility as an evidence of leisure."
> "But it is also a weapon, and it meets a felt need of barbarian man on that ground."
> "The handling of so tangible and primitive a means of offense is very comforting to any one who is gifted with even a moderate share of ferocity."
