### 核心表达

　　社会制度随环境变化而演变，但演变的速度取决于各阶级受环境压力的程度。wealthy leisure class 因被隔离于经济变革压力之外，其习惯与制度具有更强的保守性，从而延缓社会整体转型。

### 依据与细节

　　作者在第8章开头将社会结构演化比作自然选择：制度是习惯的产物，"adapted to past circumstances, and are therefore never in full accord with the requirements of the present"。适应永远落后于环境变化。制度本身构成保守因素，即"social inertia, psychological inertia, conservatism"。社会进步取决于个体在环境压力下的思想调整。关键在于："If any portion or class of society is sheltered from the action of the environment in any essential respect, that portion... will adapt its views and its scheme of life more tardily to the altered general situation; it will in so far tend to retard the process of social transformation"。作者明确指出，"The wealthy leisure class is in such a sheltered position with respect to the economic forces that make for change and readjustment"；在现代工业社会中，推动制度调整的力量"almost entirely of an economic nature"。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未具体说明 leisure class 在哪些制度领域（如政治、法律、教育）保守性最强，也未讨论其内部改革者或慈善活动的作用。该主张是结构性概括。

### 原文摘录

> "Institutions are products of the past process, are adapted to past circumstances, and are therefore never in full accord with the requirements of the present."
> "If any portion or class of society is sheltered from the action of the environment in any essential respect, that portion of the community, or that class, will adapt its views and its scheme of life more tardily to the altered general situation."
> "The wealthy leisure class is in such a sheltered position with respect to the economic forces that make for change and readjustment."
