### 核心表达

　　Deacon S. V. White 收到"H. O. Havemeyer 正在买入 Sugar"的 tip 后，没有直接跟进，而是先卖出万股测试市场承接力；看到卖单被轻易吸收后，再卖出万股确认；最终反手买入三万股并奖励 tipster 五百股。他用实际行动验证信息，而非盲目相信。

### 依据与细节

　　一位熟人激动地告诉 Deacon，Havemeyer 在买入 Sugar，预计至少涨三十点。Deacon  calmly 让经纪人 Frank 卖出十万股 Sugar。tipster 震惊。Deacon 解释："I did not doubt that you were telling me the truth as you saw it. But even if I had heard H. O. Havemeyer tell you himself, I still would have acted as I did." 第一万股卖得很顺利，不够结论性；第二万股被市场不跌反涨地吸收，这证明确实有人愿意承接所有卖盘。于是 Deacon 回补空单并买入三万股。他告诉 tipster："The way the twenty thousand shares were taken by somebody proved to me that somebody was in truth willing to take all the stock that was offered." 最后他给了 tipster 五百股作为奖励。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未说明这种测试方法在流动性不足或信息虚假时的风险；其成功依赖于 Deacon 的交易规模与市场地位。

### 原文摘录

> “I wish you’d go over to the Board and sell ten thousand Sugar.”
> “I did not doubt that you were telling me the truth as you saw it. But even if I had heard H. O. Havemeyer tell you himself, I still would have acted as I did.”
> “The first ten thousand shares went fairly easily. It was not quite conclusive. But the second ten thousand was absorbed by a market that did not stop rising.”
> “So I have covered my shorts and am long ten thousand shares, and I think that your information was good as far as it went.”
