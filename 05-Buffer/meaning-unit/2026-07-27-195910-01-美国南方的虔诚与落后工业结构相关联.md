### 核心表达

　　美国南方的宗教虔诚高于北方，这与南方较 primitive 的工业组织、较多的 mastery/subservience 关系以及相应的 archaic 生活方式相关。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，除南方外，美国几乎没有重要的 hereditary leisure class，而"This Southern leisure class is somewhat given to devout observances; more so than any class of corresponding pecuniary standing in other parts of the country"。南方的信条也"of a more old-fashioned cast"。这种更 archaic 的宗教生活与"the lower industrial development of that section"相对应。南方工业组织"of a more primitive character"，更接近手工业，机械器具少而粗糙，"more of the element of mastery and subservience"。作者列举南方更普遍的 archaic 行为：duels, brawls, feuds, drunkenness, horse-racing, cock-fighting, gambling, male sexual incontinence，以及"a livelier sense of honor"——后者是 sportsmanship 与掠夺生活的派生物。

### 限制与边界

　　该分析将南方社会特征主要归因于工业发展阶段，忽略了奴隶制历史、种植园经济、区域文化、战后重建等独立因素。其列举的"archaic offenses"带有强烈的区域偏见。

### 原文摘录

> "This Southern leisure class is somewhat given to devout observances; more so than any class of corresponding pecuniary standing in other parts of the country."
> "The industrial organization of the South is at present... of a more primitive character than that of the American community taken as a whole."
> "Among this population offenses of an archaic character also are and have been relatively more prevalent and are less deprecated than they are elsewhere."
