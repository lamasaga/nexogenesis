### 核心表达

　　现代人超出生理舒适所需的支出，主要不是为了有意识地胜过他人，而是为了符合体面的金钱生活标准。这一标准可以无限向上延伸，但一旦形成习惯便极难回缩。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，"the proximate ground of expenditure in excess of what is required for physical comfort is not a conscious effort to excel... so much as it is a desire to live up to the conventional standard of decency"。该标准 flexible and indefinitely extensible：收入增加后，只要有时间养成习惯，支出规模就会随之扩大。相反，"it is much more difficult to recede from a scale of expenditure once adopted than it is to extend the accustomed scale in response to an accession of wealth"。许多 wasteful 支出一旦纳入体面消费标准，就变得与维持生命健康的支出一样难以割舍，甚至"more indispensable"。回缩困难是道德性的，而降低低标准则可能直接减少物质舒适。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论收入骤减、经济危机或社会运动如何打破这种向下刚性。该模型描述的是常规状态下的消费习惯惯性。

### 原文摘录

> "The standard is flexible; and especially it is indefinitely extensible, if only time is allowed for habituation to any increase in pecuniary ability."
> "It is much more difficult to recede from a scale of expenditure once adopted than it is to extend the accustomed scale in response to an accession of wealth."
> "It is notoriously just as difficult to recede from a 'high' standard of living as it is to lower a standard which is already relatively low."
