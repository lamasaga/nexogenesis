### 核心表达

　　宗教建筑与仪式用品必须显得昂贵且浪费，但又要刻意避免给崇拜者带来舒适，以符合代理性消费的声誉逻辑。这种"苦行式奢华"是炫耀性浪费原则在神圣领域的直接延伸。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，炫耀性浪费原则解释了"a great portion of what may be called devout consumption"，包括教堂建筑、祭服等。即便那些宣称神偏好"temples not built with hands"的现代教派，其圣所仍以体面程度的浪费来建造装饰。昂贵辉煌对崇拜者有"uplifting and mellowing effect"，而任何贫困或简陋的证据都会令旁观者感到羞耻。关键规则是："The accessories of any devout observance should be pecuniarily above reproach"。更值得注意的是，神圣空间不仅较少服务于身体舒适，而且"anything that might serve the comfort of the worshipper should be conspicuously absent"；即便存在舒适元素，也必须伪装在表面的苦行之下。作者解释，这种 devout austerity 基于两个原则：炫耀性浪费的金钱声誉，以及代理性消费不应增进消费者自身舒适。

### 限制与边界

　　作者区分了两种崇拜：一种认为神亲自使用并享受奢侈品，其圣所更接近世俗主人的炫耀性消费；另一种认为圣物只是为神服务的工具，其特征是不增进使用者舒适。该主张主要覆盖有神人格化的宗教传统。

### 原文摘录

> "Even in those modern cults to whose divinities is imputed a predilection for temples not built with hands, the sacred buildings and the other properties of the cult are constructed and decorated with some view to a reputable degree of wasteful expenditure."
> "The accessories of any devout observance should be pecuniarily above reproach. This requirement is imperative, whatever latitude may be allowed with regard to these accessories in point of aesthetic or other serviceability."
> "This canon of devout austerity is based on the pecuniary reputability of conspicuously wasteful consumption, backed by the principle that vicarious consumption should conspicuously not conduce to the comfort of the vicarious consumer."
