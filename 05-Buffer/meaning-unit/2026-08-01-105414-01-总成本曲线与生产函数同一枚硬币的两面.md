Total-cost curve 随产量上升 **gets steeper**；production function 随雇工增加 **gets flatter**——同一机制的两面。高产量意味着厨房拥挤；因 diminishing marginal product，**each additional worker adds little to production**（生产函数趋平）。反过来，拥挤时 **producing an additional cookie requires a lot more labor, which is costly**（总成本曲线趋陡）。理解该镜像关系是连接「技术约束」与「成本约束」的枢纽。

> These two curves are opposite sides of the same coin.
