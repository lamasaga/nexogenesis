### 核心表达

　　Thomas Jefferson 在1783年重返国会后，提出并推动建立了美国联邦十进制货币体系，取代英制镑、先令、便士，并建议在费城建造铸币厂——这是联邦政府建造的第一座公共建筑。

### 依据与细节

　　- Jefferson 在1783年返回国会，报告了1783年9月3日巴黎和约。
- 他“proposed and carried through Congress a bill establishing the present Federal system of coinage, which took the place of the English pounds, shillings, pence, etc.”
- 他还引入了“measures for establishing a Mint in Philadelphia”，并指出这是“the first public building built by the general Government, still standing on Seventh street, east side, near Filbert”。
- 该传记将 Jefferson 的货币改革与其在弗吉尼亚州废除限嗣继承、长子继承制、推进宗教自由和公共教育等改革并列。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提及：Jefferson 的货币提案是否受到欧洲模式（如法国十进制）影响、国会辩论细节、以及他本人的具体论点。本片段为简短人物传记，非专题政策分析。

### 原文摘录

> "He also proposed and carried through Congress a bill establishing the present Federal system of coinage, which took the place of the English pounds, shillings, pence, etc., and also introduced measures for establishing a Mint in Philadelphia, (the first public building built by the general Government, still standing on Seventh street, east side, near Filbert)."
