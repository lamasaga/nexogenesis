### 核心表达

　　现代女装的高跟鞋、长裙、束身衣等不便设计，是社会在理论上仍将女性视为男性经济依附者乃至财产的标志；女性服装的功能在于为家庭展示支付能力，而非服务于穿戴者自身舒适或声誉。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，女性承担为家庭 head 进行代理性消费的职能，"her apparel is contrived with this object in view"。明显的生产劳动对体面女性尤其有辱身份，因此女装特别着力于展示"the wearer does not and can not habitually engage in useful work"。与男性不同，女性被期待"abstain more consistently from useful effort and to make more of a show of leisure"。作者进一步论证，若某种浪费/闲暇展示达到明显不适或自愿致残的程度，则穿戴者并非为个人金钱声誉，而是为代表其经济依附的他人服务。因此，高跟鞋、长裙、不实用的 bonnet、束身衣等"are so many items of evidence to the effect that in the modern civilized scheme of life the woman is still, in theory, the economic dependent of the man"。作者甚至称，在金钱文化的理想方案中，"the lady of the house is the chief menial of the household"。

### 限制与边界

　　该论断高度依赖其理论框架中的"经济依附"定义。原文未考虑女性通过自身劳动获得独立收入后的着装变化，也未讨论现代女性职业装的出现。

### 原文摘录

> "Her sphere is within the household, which she should 'beautify,' and of which she should be the 'chief ornament.'"
> "By virtue of its descent from a patriarchal past, our social system makes it the woman's function in an especial degree to put in evidence her household's ability to pay."
> "The homely reason for all this conspicuous leisure and attire on the part of women lies in the fact that they are servants to whom... has been delegated the office of putting in evidence their master's ability to pay."
