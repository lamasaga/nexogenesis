### 核心表达

　　真正的交易是一个人的生意：价格要么按你判断的方向走，要么反向走，朋友或伙伴的善意都无法改变；因此无需向他人透露自己的交易。

### 依据与细节

　　作者说他没有追随者，业务保密。"It was my head, wasn’t it? Prices either were going the way I doped them out, without any help from friends or partners, or they were going the other way, and nobody could stop them out of kindness to me." 他因此被称为 Boy Plunger，bucket shop 因他持续赢钱而拒绝接受他的生意。他不得不使用假名、更换经纪商，有时故意先输以麻痹对方。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论机构交易、团队合作或信息合法共享的情形；其独行侠立场基于个人经验与 bucket shop 环境。

### 原文摘录

> I didn’t have a following. I kept my business to myself. It was a one-man business, anyhow.
> I’ve got friends, of course, but my business has always been the same--a one-man affair. That is why I have always played a lone hand.
> They’d tell me there was nothing doing. That was the time they got to calling me the Boy Plunger.
