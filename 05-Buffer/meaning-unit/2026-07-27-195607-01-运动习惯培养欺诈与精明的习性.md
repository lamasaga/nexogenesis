### 核心表达

　　策略、欺诈与恐吓是体育比赛的内置方法；长期参与运动会使人习惯于 overreach 对手，并对他人利益漠不关心，从而强化狭隘的自我中心习惯。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，prowess 在野蛮生活中表现为 force and fraud 两个方向，现代战争、金钱职业、体育游戏中同样存在这两种形式。体育与更严肃的 emulation 生活都培养和强化这两类能力。"Strategy or cunning is an element invariably present in games, as also in warlike pursuits and in the chase." 策略会发展为 finesse and chicanery。"Chicanery, falsehood, browbeating, hold a well-secured place in the method of procedure of any athletic contest and in games generally"。裁判的存在与详细的规则充分证明，"fraudulent practices and attempts to overreach one's opponents are not adventitious features of the game"。因此，habituation to sports 会促进 fraud 能力的发展；社会中倾向于体育的掠夺气质盛行，意味着"sharp practice and callous disregard of the interests of others, individually and collectively"的盛行。

### 限制与边界

　　作者将规则内的策略等同于欺诈，这一判断具有争议。现代体育伦理明确区分合规策略与欺诈作弊，而作者将其连续统化。

### 原文摘录

> "Chicanery, falsehood, browbeating, hold a well-secured place in the method of procedure of any athletic contest and in games generally."
> "The habitual employment of an umpire, and the minute technical regulations governing the limits and details of permissible fraud and strategic advantage, sufficiently attest the fact that fraudulent practices and attempts to overreach one's opponents are not adventitious features of the game."
> "Resort to fraud, in any guise and under any legitimation of law or custom, is an expression of a narrowly self-regarding habit of mind."
