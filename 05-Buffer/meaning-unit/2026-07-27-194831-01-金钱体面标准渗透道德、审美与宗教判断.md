### 核心表达

　　炫耀性浪费不仅决定消费数量与种类，还通过塑造思维习惯，渗透到人们对诚实、美、效用、宗教仪式乃至科学真理的判断标准中。

### 依据与细节

　　作者在第6章开头指出，消费者通常并非直接受"赤裸裸的炫耀性浪费原则"驱动，而是希望"conform to established usage"、避免非议。然而，这种遵从会反过来"affect the habitual view of what is good and right in life in other directions"。经济兴趣并非孤立存在，"the principle of conspicuous waste guides the formation of habits of thought as to what is honest and reputable in life and in commodities"，并会 traverse（横切）其他行为规范，包括"the sense of duty, the sense of beauty, the sense of utility, the sense of devotional or ritualistic fitness, and the scientific sense of truth"。即使是内衣、食物、厨具等不对外展示的用品，也可能包含增加成本却不同比增加实用性的特征，说明规范已内化为消费结构的一部分。

### 限制与边界

　　原文承认这些渗透作用是"indirect and remoter"，并预告后续章节将分别讨论其对科学精神与宗教仪式的影响。该主张是纲领性的，而非详细论证。

### 原文摘录

> "The principle of conspicuous waste guides the formation of habits of thought as to what is honest and reputable in life and in commodities."
> "So the canon of honorific waste may, immediately or remotely, influence the sense of duty, the sense of beauty, the sense of utility, the sense of devotional or ritualistic fitness, and the scientific sense of truth."
> "In all such useful articles a close scrutiny will discover certain features which add to the cost and enhance the commercial value of the goods in question, but do not proportionately increase the serviceability of these articles for the material purposes which alone they ostensibly are designed to serve."
