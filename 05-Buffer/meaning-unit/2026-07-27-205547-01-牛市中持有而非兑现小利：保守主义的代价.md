### 核心表达

　　在牛市中，频繁兑现小利并等待回调再买入的"明智保守"策略，往往使交易者错失主要涨幅；真正的大钱来自持有主要头寸直到牛市接近尾声。

### 依据与细节

　　作者回忆自己从牛市一开始就看多并买入，行情如期上涨。但他听从老手建议，决定"wise and play carefully, conservatively"——获利了结，等回调再买入。结果回调常常不来，股票继续上涨十点，而他只守住四点利润。他指出："They say you never grow poor taking profits. No, you don’t. But neither do you grow rich taking a four-point profit in a bull market." 本该赚两万美元的地方只赚了两千美元。这种"半吊子 sucker"（semisucker）知道所有规则，却不懂牛市中该持有。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论在趋势反转时如何识别终点；其教训针对趋势市场中的过度保守。

### 原文摘录

> I listened to the elder statesmen and curbed my youthful impetuousness. I made up my mind to be wise and play carefully, conservatively.
> I often took profits and waited for a reaction that never came. And I saw my stock go kiting up ten points more and I sitting there with my four-point profit safe in my conservative pocket.
> They say you never grow poor taking profits. No, you don’t. But neither do you grow rich taking a four-point profit in a bull market.
> Where I should have made twenty thousand dollars I made two thousand.
