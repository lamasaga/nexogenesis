### 核心表达

　　女装比男装更进一步地证明穿戴者脱离生产劳动：高跟鞋、长裙、长发、束身衣等不仅是昂贵的装饰，更是直接削弱甚至致残穿戴者劳动能力的装置。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，"The dress of women goes even farther than that of men in the way of demonstrating the wearer's abstinence from productive employment"。女性的高顶 bonnet 比男性高帽更妨碍工作；女性皮鞋加上 French heel，"obviously makes any, even the simplest and most necessary manual work extremely difficult"。长裙"hamper the wearer at every turn and incapacitates her for all useful exertion"，这是人们顽固依恋裙子的实质原因。长发同样如此。最具特征的是束身衣（corset）：在经济理论上，它"substantially a mutilation, undergone for the purpose of lowering the subject's vitality and rendering her permanently and obviously unfit for work"。虽然束身衣可能损害个人吸引力，但"the loss suffered on that score is offset by the gain in reputability which comes of her visibly increased expensiveness and infirmity"。作者总结，女性服装的女性气质实质上归结为"the more effective hindrance to useful exertion offered by the garments peculiar to women"。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论女性对自身服饰选择的能动性、舒适性或身份表达需求。该解释强调服装作为社会信号的功能，而非穿着者主体经验。

### 原文摘录

> "The dress of women goes even farther than that of men in the way of demonstrating the wearer's abstinence from productive employment."
> "The corset is, in economic theory, substantially a mutilation, undergone for the purpose of lowering the subject's vitality and rendering her permanently and obviously unfit for work."
> "It may broadly be set down that the womanliness of woman's apparel resolves itself, in point of substantial fact, into the more effective hindrance to useful exertion offered by the garments peculiar to women."
