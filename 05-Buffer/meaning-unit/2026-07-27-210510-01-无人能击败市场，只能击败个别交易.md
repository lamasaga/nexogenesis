### 核心表达

　　一个人可能在某个时间击败某只股票或某个商品，但没有人能击败整个股票市场或整个商品市场。这就像赛马：可以赢一场比赛，但无法击败赛马本身。

### 依据与细节

　　作者在交易近三十年后得出结论："A man may beat a stock or a group at a certain time, but no man living can beat the stock market! A man may make money out of individual deals in cotton or grain, but no man can beat the cotton market or the grain market." 他将此比作赛马：可以击败一匹马，但无法击败赛马运动。他强调如果有人能反驳，他愿意把话说得更强烈。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未定义"击败市场"的具体含义（如持续跑赢指数）；其论断带有经验性概括色彩。

### 原文摘录

> A man may beat a stock or a group at a certain time, but no man living can beat the stock market! A man may make money out of individual deals in cotton or grain, but no man can beat the cotton market or the grain market.
> It’s like the track. A man may beat a horse race, but he cannot beat horse racing.
> I know I am right in saying these are incontrovertible statements.
