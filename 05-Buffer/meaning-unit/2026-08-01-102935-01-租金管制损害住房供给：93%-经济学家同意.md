命题「**A ceiling on rents reduces the quantity and quality of housing available**」经民调获 **93 percent** 经济学家同意。Rent control 对 landlords 可收租金设 legal maximum，经济学家认为其 adversely affects housing supply，是以 costly way 帮助 neediest members。尽管如此，许多 city governments 仍 ignore 经济学家建议而实施租金上限。
