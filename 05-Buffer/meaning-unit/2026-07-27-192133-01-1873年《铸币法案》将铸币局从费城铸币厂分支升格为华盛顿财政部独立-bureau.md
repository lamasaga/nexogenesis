### 核心表达

　　1873年《铸币法案》不仅是技术性的法律整理，更将美国铸币局从费城“母铸币厂”总监下辖的机构重组为华盛顿财政部的独立 bureau，由 Director of the Mints 统管全国铸币厂与化验所，反映了联邦政府货币管理职能的中央化与专业化。

### 依据与细节

　　- 此前，费城铸币厂总监（Director of the Mint at Philadelphia）是所有铸币厂的总监，旧金山、新奥尔良等称为 Branch Mints，各分支主管直接向费城汇报。
- 1873年法案的起草者认为铸币服务已成为政府中“so large and important a part”，应单独设为财政部 bureau，总监驻华盛顿。
- 法案将80多年60多项铸币法律整理为修订法典中约8-10页。
- 1870年 Knox 向国会提交的报告（Senate Mis. Doc. No. 132, XLI. Cong., 2d Sess.）是法案基础；报告先以宽边距印刷分发各铸币厂和专家征求意见，再据此修改。
- 新法下首任 Director of the Mint 为 Henry R. Linderman；费城首席官员职称改为 Superintendent，首任为 James Pollock。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提及：重组是否遭到费城铸币厂抵制、预算与人员编制变化、以及华盛顿 bureau 与费城 Superintendent 之间的实际权力分配。

### 原文摘录

> "Prior to this, the Director of the Mint at Philadelphia was the Director of all the Mints—the institution at Philadelphia being regarded as the 'Mother Mint,' and the others, at San Francisco, New Orleans, etc., were called Branch Mints."
> "But the authors of the Act of 1873 regarded the Mint Service as so large and important a part of the Government, that it should be constituted a separate Bureau of the Treasury, with the Director located at Washington."
> "The bill, which comprised within the compass of eight or ten pages of the Revised Statutes every important provision contained in more than sixty different enactments upon the Mint and Coinage of the United States—the result of eighty years of legislation..."
