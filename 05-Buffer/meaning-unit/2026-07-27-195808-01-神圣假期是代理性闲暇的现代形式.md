### 核心表达

　　神圣节日要求人们停止有用劳动，这实质上是向超自然 leisure class 缴纳代理性闲暇的贡品；世俗节日也源于此，包括为劳动本身设立的 Labor Day。

### 依据与细节

　　作者认为，神圣假日"obviously to be construed as a season of vicarious leisure performed for the divinity or saint in whose name the tabu is imposed"。其核心特征是"a more or less rigid tabu on all activity that is of human use"。斋戒日进一步强化这一点：不仅停止生产性劳动，还强制停止有助于舒适或充实的消费。世俗节日同样源于此：从真正的神圣日，经过国王与伟人生日等半神圣日，到为重大事件或事实设立的纪念日。作者以 Labor Day 为典型例子："A day of vicarious leisure has in some communities been set apart as Labor Day"。这一做法试图"augment the prestige of the fact of labor, by the archaic, predatory method of a compulsory abstention from useful effort"。"Such a tithe of vicarious leisure is a perquisite of all members of the preternatural leisure class and is indispensable to their good fame."

### 限制与边界

　　作者将 Labor Day 的设立动机还原为声誉机制，未讨论劳动者争取休息权的真实政治斗争。该解释具有强烈的还原论色彩。

### 原文摘录

> "Sacred holidays are obviously to be construed as a season of vicarious leisure performed for the divinity or saint in whose name the tabu is imposed."
> "A day of vicarious leisure has in some communities been set apart as Labor Day. This observance is designed to augment the prestige of the fact of labor, by the archaic, predatory method of a compulsory abstention from useful effort."
> "Un saint qu'on ne chôme pas is indeed a saint fallen on evil days."
