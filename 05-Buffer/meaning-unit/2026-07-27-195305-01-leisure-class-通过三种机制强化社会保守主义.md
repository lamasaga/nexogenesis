### 核心表达

　　leisure class 延缓文化发展的机制有三：其一，阶级自身因免于经济压力而具有惯性；其二，其保守的示范作用使下层阶级也趋于保守；其三，财富不平等使下层阶级缺乏改变习惯所需的剩余精力。

### 依据与细节

　　作者明确指出，leisure class 的保守性"by no means novel"，但需要从结构性位置而非恶意动机来理解。第一，该阶级被 shelter 于经济压力之外，"not required under penalty of forfeiture to change their habits of life"，因此缺乏变革动力。第二，其规范成为全社会体面的标准，"It makes it incumbent upon all reputable people to follow their lead"，从而"stiffen the resistance of all other classes against any innovation"。第三，财富向上集中意味着下层被剥夺 sustenance，"reducing their consumption, and consequently their available energy, to such a point as to make them incapable of the effort required for the learning and adoption of new habits of thought"。此外还可补充阶级利益：leisure class 处于特权地位，"any departure from the existing order may be expected to work to the detriment of the class"。

### 限制与边界

　　该模型未讨论 leisure class 内部的改革派、慈善家或知识分子的作用，也未量化三种机制的相对重要性。它是对阶级保守功能的结构性描述。

### 原文摘录

> "The leisure class is the conservative class."
> "The office of the leisure class in social evolution is to retard the movement and to conserve what is obsolescent."
> "The institution of a leisure class hinders cultural development immediately (1) by the inertia proper to the class itself, (2) through its prescriptive example of conspicuous waste and of conservatism, and (3) indirectly through that system of unequal distribution of wealth and sustenance on which the institution itself rests."
