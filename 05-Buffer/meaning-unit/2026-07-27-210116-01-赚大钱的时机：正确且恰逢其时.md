### 核心表达

　　赚钱的方法是去赚；赚大钱的办法是在准确的时间点正确判断。投机者必须既是学生又是实践者，理论必须与操作结合。

### 依据与细节

　　作者在 1907 年 2 月后约四个月熊市交易中获利，但承认战术上仍有不足——没有严格坐住，而是不断回补再卖出。到夏天市场清淡，他清仓去欧洲，身价超过七十五万美元。他在 Aix-les-Bains 度假时，从《巴黎先驱报》读到 Smelters 宣布额外股息的消息，立刻意识到这是牛市集团 desperate 地对抗基本条件。他发电报做空 Smelters，并提前一个月回纽约。作者说："The way to make money is to make it. The way to make big money is to be right at exactly the right time. In this business a man has to think of both theory and practice. A speculator must not be merely a student, he must be both a student and a speculator."

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提供识别"准确时机"的客观标准；其成功仍依赖经验与直觉。

### 原文摘录

> The way to make money is to make it. The way to make big money is to be right at exactly the right time.
> In this business a man has to think of both theory and practice. A speculator must not be merely a student, he must be both a student and a speculator.
> Tape reading was an important part of the game; so was beginning at the right time; so was sticking to your position. But my greatest discovery was that a man must study general conditions, to size them so as to be able to anticipate probabilities.
