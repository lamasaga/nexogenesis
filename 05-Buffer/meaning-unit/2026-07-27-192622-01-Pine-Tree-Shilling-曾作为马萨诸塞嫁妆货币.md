### 核心表达

　　马萨诸塞殖民地 Pine Tree Shilling 不仅是流通货币，还进入民间仪式：John Hall 女儿的嫁妆按其体重以 Pine Tree Shillings 支付，反映早期殖民硬币在地方社会中的储蓄与象征功能。

### 依据与细节

　　- 脚注 [14] 原文提到“the old story of the weighing of John Hall's daughter on her marriage-day”：其嫁妆为“her weight in 'pine-tree' shillings”。
- 本书作者暗示现存某些 Pine Tree Shilling 标本可能曾是该新娘嫁妆的一部分。

### 限制与边界

　　原文称此为“old story”并带推测语气（"the suggestion is allowable"），其历史真实性未经考证。

### 原文摘录

> "The old story of the weighing of John Hall's daughter on her marriage-day is recalled in seeing these coins. Her dowry was her weight in 'pine-tree' shillings; and the suggestion is allowable that these specimens formed a part of the portion of the blushing bride two centuries ago."
