### 核心表达

　　作者的交易理论之一是：当股票首次突破 100、200、300 等整数关口时，价格通常不会停在该整数，而会继续上涨相当幅度；因此在突破线买入几乎肯定会显示利润。

### 依据与细节

　　1907 年 2 月作者在佛罗里达度假，看到市场大涨十多点后决定上岸查看。他在 Palm Beach 的 Harding 分公司看到 Anaconda 即将突破 300。Anaconda 是 quarter stock（面值 25 美元），四百股才等于普通一百股。作者判断它突破 300 后应继续上行，可能很快触及 340。他解释："It was an old trading theory of mine that when a stock crosses 100 or 200 or 300 for the first time the price does not stop at the even figure but goes a good deal higher, so that if you buy it as soon as it crosses the line it is almost certain to show you a profit." 胆怯者不喜欢在历史新高买入，但历史运动支持这一做法。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提供该理论的统计验证；在当代市场中，整数关口的心理效应已因算法交易而复杂化。

### 原文摘录

> It was an old trading theory of mine that when a stock crosses 100 or 200 or 300 for the first time the price does not stop at the even figure but goes a good deal higher, so that if you buy it as soon as it crosses the line it is almost certain to show you a profit.
> Timid people don’t like to buy a stock at a new high record. But I had the history of such movements to guide me.
> Anaconda was only quarter stock... I figured that when it crossed 300 it ought to keep on going and probably touch 340 in a jiffy.
