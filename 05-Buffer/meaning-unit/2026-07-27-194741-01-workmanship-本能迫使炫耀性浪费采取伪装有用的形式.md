### 核心表达

　　人类普遍具有 workmanship 本能，即对生产效率和实际用途的偏好。该本能与炫耀性浪费相冲突，但并未消除浪费，而是迫使浪费披上"看似有用"的外衣，表现为社交义务、准艺术活动、慈善组织等形式。

### 依据与细节

　　作者称 workmanship 为"alien factor"，它"disposes men to look with favor upon productive efficiency and on whatever is of human use"，并"deprecate waste of substance or effort"。这种本能即使在炫耀性浪费占上风的社会中也存在，因此任何浪费性支出都需至少具备"colorable excuse in the way of an ostensible purpose"。在准和平阶段，奴隶制与身份制度压制了这种本能；进入和平工业阶段后，"the instinct comes more effectively into play"。现代闲暇阶级为了既满足对有用活动的冲动，又不违反"生产性劳动有失体面"的规范，发展出大量"make-believe"活动："social duties"、"quasi-artistic or quasi-scholarly accomplishments"、家居装饰、缝纫小组、服饰改良、游艇、高尔夫等。作者以母鸡孵瓷蛋比喻：这些活动可能流于荒谬，但不能因此否认 workmanship 本能的真实存在。

### 限制与边界

　　作者将 workmanship 视为一种本能，但未给出心理学或生物学证据。该模型用于解释现代社会中"忙碌而无用"的仪式性活动，而非所有休闲或艺术活动。

### 原文摘录

> "Other circumstances permitting, that instinct disposes men to look with favor upon productive efficiency and on whatever is of human use. It disposes them to deprecate waste of substance or effort."
> "The consequence is that a change has been wrought in the conspicuous leisure practiced by the leisure class; not so much in substance as in form. A reconciliation between the two conflicting requirements is effected by a resort to make-believe."
> "The fact that it may under stress of circumstances eventuate in inanities no more disproves the presence of the instinct than the reality of the brooding instinct is disproved by inducing a hen to sit on a nestful of china eggs."
