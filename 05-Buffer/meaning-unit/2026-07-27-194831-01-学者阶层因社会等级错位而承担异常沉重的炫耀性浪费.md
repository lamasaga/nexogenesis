### 核心表达

　　学者阶层因被社会归入高于其金钱收入的等级，必须遵循更高的金钱体面标准，结果在炫耀性浪费上支出比例极高，挤压了家庭舒适、子女养育等其他生活目标。

### 依据与细节

　　作者观察到，学者阶层"by convention subsumed under a higher social grade than their pecuniary grade should warrant"，因为他们被认为拥有稀缺的精神天赋。于是"The scale of decent expenditure in their case is pitched correspondingly high"，留给其他生活目的的余裕异常狭窄。他们不可避免地与金钱地位更高的阶层接触，后者的高标准被"transfused among the scholarly classes with but little mitigation of its rigor"。因此，"there is no class of the community that spends a larger proportion of its substance in conspicuous waste than these"。作者还指出，这种机制导致家庭内部消费相对寒酸，私人生活被遮蔽，以及低出生率——因为要体面地养育一个孩子所需的可观炫耀性消费构成了"the most effectual of the Malthusian prudential checks"。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提供学者阶层与其他阶层消费比例的实证数据。该论断基于作者对社会等级与体面标准关系的推理。

### 原文摘录

> "Because of a presumed superiority and scarcity of the gifts and attainments that characterize their life, these classes are by convention subsumed under a higher social grade than their pecuniary grade should warrant."
> "There is no class of the community that spends a larger proportion of its substance in conspicuous waste than these."
> "The low birthrate of the classes upon whom the requirements of reputable expenditure fall with great urgency is likewise traceable to the exigencies of a standard of living based on conspicuous waste."
