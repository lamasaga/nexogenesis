### 核心表达

　　机器制商品往往在物质功能上更完善，却因成本低廉、人人可得而被视为礼仪上低劣。手工制品的可见粗糙与不完美反而成为荣誉性标志，证明其生产耗费了更多劳动且非普通人所能拥有。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，机器产品与手工产品的物质差异在于："the former serve their primary purpose more adequately. They are a more perfect product--show a more perfect adaptation of means to end"。但机器产品"fall short under the test of honorific waste"。手工劳动是更浪费的生产方式，因此手工制品"more serviceable for the purpose of pecuniary reputability"。手工制品的荣誉性标志通常是某些不完美与不规则，"showing where the workman has fallen short in the execution of the design"。这种优越性建立在"a certain margin of crudeness"之上：粗糙度不能太宽以致显得笨拙（暗示低成本），也不能太窄以致像机器一样精确（暗示低成本）。机器产品因其"commonness"——即在金钱上为多数人所及——而带来"odious suggestion of the lower levels of human life"，令敏感者感到卑劣与压抑。

### 限制与边界

　　作者承认 workmanship 本能也会使人们对精湛工艺产生欣赏，但长期而言，任何工艺特征若无炫耀性浪费的认可，便无法被现代文明消费者接受。

### 原文摘录

> "Hand labor is a more wasteful method of production; hence the goods turned out by this method are more serviceable for the purpose of pecuniary reputability."
> "The ground of the superiority of hand-wrought goods, therefore, is a certain margin of crudeness."
> "What is common is within the (pecuniary) reach of many people. Its consumption is therefore not honorific, since it does not serve the purpose of a favorable invidious comparison with other consumers."
