### 核心表达

　　取消一切特惠或限制后，**自然自由体系**自行确立；君主只需履行三项明确职责：**国防**、**司法公正**、**公共工程与机构**。

### 依据与细节

- 君主不应监督或指导私人产业，因为无人能具备足够智慧完成此任。
- 三项职责：保护社会免受外敌暴力与侵略；保护社会成员免受彼此不公与压迫；建立和维护个人或小团体无利可图但社会整体受益的公共工程与机构。
- 履行这些职责需要支出，因而需要收入；后续将讨论支出的分担方式、社会整体贡献方式以及公共债务。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提及：未界定“公共工程”的具体范围；未说明在多大程度上可允许例外或监管。

### 原文摘录

> "All systems, either of preference or of restraint, therefore, being thus completely taken away, the obvious and simple system of natural liberty establishes itself of its own accord."

> "The sovereign is completely discharged from a duty... the duty of superintending the industry of private people, and of directing it towards the employments most suitable to the interests of the society."

> "According to the system of natural liberty, the sovereign has only three duties to attend to... first, the duty of protecting the society... secondly, the duty of protecting... every member... from the injustice or oppression of every other member... and, thirdly, the duty of erecting and maintaining certain public works..."
