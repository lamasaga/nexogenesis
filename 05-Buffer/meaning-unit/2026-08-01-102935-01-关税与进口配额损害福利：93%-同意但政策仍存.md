命题「**Tariffs and import quotas usually reduce general economic welfare**」再次 **93 percent** 同意。经济学家 oppose **tariffs**（进口税）与 **import quotas**（进口数量上限），因这些政策 impede **specialization** 从而损害国内外 living standards。然而 presidents and Congress 多年来仍 often restrict imports。政策持续或因 **realities of the political process** 为 immovable obstacles，或因经济学家 **have not yet convinced enough of the public**——本书目的之一即帮助读者理解并 perhaps 接受 economist's view。
