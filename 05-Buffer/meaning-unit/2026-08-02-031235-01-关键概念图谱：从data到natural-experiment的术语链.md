本章术语链把数据工作串成一条可检索路径：data（事实信息基础）→ econometrics（发展分析工具的子领域）→ randomized controlled trial / experimental data vs observational data；观察侧陷阱 confounding variable 与 reverse causality；数据结构 cross-sectional / time-series / panel；分析目标侧 parameters；估计侧 linear regression、ordinary least squares、standard error；处理混淆的 multiple regression；识别因果的 natural experiment。这些概念对应正文机制而非孤立词表，复习时应连回「实验vs观察→描述/量化/检验/预测→OLS与标准误→多元回归与自然实验/工具变量」的叙事。

> data; econometrics; RCT; experimental/observational data; confounding; reverse causality; cross-section/time-series/panel; parameters; linear regression; OLS; standard error; multiple regression; natural experiment
