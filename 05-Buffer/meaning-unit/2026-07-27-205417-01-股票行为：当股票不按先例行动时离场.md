### 核心表达

　　投机不仅是数学或规则；股票有"行为"，其行为可帮助判断它是否会按观察到的先例继续。如果一只股票行为不对，就不要碰它——因为无法精确诊断问题，就无法预测方向；没有诊断就没有预后，没有预后就没有利润。

### 依据与细节

　　作者认为平均的"ticker hound"或"tape-worm"犯错多因过度专门化（over-specialization）导致高度昂贵的不弹性。投机虽有刚性法则，但不是纯数学。作者提到"the behavior of a stock, actions that enable you to judge whether or not it is going to proceed in accordance with the precedents." 他用医学比喻：无法诊断就无法预后，无法预后就无法盈利。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未给出"行为不对"的具体识别标准；其方法依赖经验性观察。

### 原文摘录

> The average ticker hound--or, as they used to call him, tape-worm--goes wrong, I suspect, as much from over-specialization as from anything else. It means a highly expensive inelasticity.
> After all, the game of speculation isn’t all mathematics or set rules, however rigid the main laws may be. Even in my tape reading something enters that is more than mere arithmetic. There is what I call the behavior of a stock.
> If a stock doesn’t act right don’t touch it; because, being unable to tell precisely what is wrong, you cannot tell which way it is going. No diagnosis, no prognosis. No prognosis, no profit.
