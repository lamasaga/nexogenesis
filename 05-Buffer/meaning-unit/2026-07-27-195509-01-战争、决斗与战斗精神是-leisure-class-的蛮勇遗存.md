### 核心表达

　　战争、决斗与习惯性战斗精神在现代社会中主要集中于 hereditary leisure class 与 lower-class delinquents，而工业大众则相对缺乏这种好战气质；这是掠夺文化在人类气质与社会制度中的遗存。

### 依据与细节

　　第10章指出，"The most immediate and unequivocal expression of that archaic human nature... is the fighting propensity proper"。这种战斗倾向在 leisure class 中程度最高，"the hereditary leisure class is endowed with this martial spirit in a higher degree than the middle classes"，且该阶级以此为荣。战争被视为 honorable，对战争勇武的钦佩本身证明钦佩者具有 predatory temperament。在和平时期，唯一能与 hereditary leisure class 争夺好战精神荣誉的是 lower-class delinquents。普通工业大众"relatively apathetic touching warlike interests"，"rather averse to any other than a defensive fight"。决斗作为 leisure-class institution，"in substance a more or less deliberate resort to a fight as a final settlement of a difference of opinion"，几乎仅在有 hereditary leisure class 的地方盛行。作者列举：高级绅士与 rowdy 是用拳脚解决意见分歧的两种人，而"the plain man will ordinarily fight only when excessive momentary irritation or alcoholic exaltation"使其暂时 revert to archaic habit。

### 限制与边界

　　该主张具有明显的时代与阶级偏见，未考虑征兵制、民族主义宣传、媒体煽动等因素对大众战争态度的影响。它也未能解释现代民主社会中战争动员的广泛社会基础。

### 原文摘录

> "War is honorable, and warlike prowess is eminently honorific in the eyes of the generality of men; and this admiration of warlike prowess is itself the best voucher of a predatory temperament in the admirer of war."
> "The duel is in substance a more or less deliberate resort to a fight as a final settlement of a difference of opinion."
> "It is only the high-bred gentleman and the rowdy that normally resort to blows as the universal solvent of differences of opinion."
