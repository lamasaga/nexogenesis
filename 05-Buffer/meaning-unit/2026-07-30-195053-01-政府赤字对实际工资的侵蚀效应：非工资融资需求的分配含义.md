### 核心表达

　　在附录 B 的框架中，政府赤字（**D_f**）的上升会降低消费品工人的价格折算工资；赤字下降则会提高之。赤字通过抢夺消费品部门的实际资源而构成对实际工资的隐性挤压。

### 依据与细节

- 方程（6）的分子中包含 **D_f + T_n - Π_G + cΠ** 等“非工资融资的消费需求”项，政府赤字是其中之一。
- 原文明确给出方向性结论：a rise in the deficit lowers and a decline raises the price deflated wages of consumer goods workers.
- 这意味着财政扩张若无相应的消费品产出增长，会以通胀或实际工资下降的形式由工资收入者承担部分成本。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提及：赤字资金的具体来源（货币化 vs 债券化）；未讨论不同收入群体对赤字的负担分摊；未给出该效应的数量弹性。

### 原文摘录

> In these equations, we have D_f = the government deficit, T_n = the taxes on profits, Π_G = the profits from producing output for the government, cΠ = consumption out of after-tax profits and sW = savings out of after-tax wages.

> Note that a rise in the deficit lowers and a decline raises the price deflated wages of consumer goods workers.

> The purchasing power of money wages is positively related to the average productivity of labor and inversely related to the size of the demands for consumption goods that are financed by other than the income derived from the production of consumer goods.
