经济学家常被要求 **explain causes**——如为何青少年 **unemployment** 高于年长工人——属 **factual question**，可 **scientifically** 回答。有时则被要求 **recommend policies** 改善 outcomes——政府应做什么提升青少年 well-being？——这除理解现状外还需 **value judgments** 关于 **what ought to be done**。2016 年《纽约时报》报道：Amazon、Facebook、Google、Microsoft、Airbnb、Uber 等 **tech companies** 以 **big data** 与高薪吸引经济学家——不同于传统宏观趋势顾问，他们研究 **consumer behavior data trails** 以优化 advertising、travel、lodging 等 **digital marketplaces**；Harvard 的 Jack Coles 称 Silicon Valley 对经济学家是 **「absolute candy store」**；Google chief economist Hal Varian、Microsoft chief economist Preston McAfee 等强调 **microeconomic experts, heavy on data and computing**。McAfee：「Economics influences rather than determines decisions.」

> When economists are trying to explain the world, they are scientists. When they are giving guidance on how to improve it, they are policy advisers.
