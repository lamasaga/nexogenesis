### 核心表达

　　在现代金钱文化中取得成功的理想类型，与罪犯在核心气质上惊人相似：二者都无情地将人和物转化为自身目的，漠视他人感受与行为的远期后果；区别在于成功者更具地位感、更坚韧、更有远见。

### 依据与细节

　　作者直接比较："The ideal pecuniary man is like the ideal delinquent in his unscrupulous conversion of goods and persons to his own ends, and in a callous disregard of the feelings and wishes of others and of the remoter effects of his actions"。二者的差异在于，理想金钱人"unlike him in possessing a keener sense of status, and in working more consistently and farsightedly to a remoter end"。两种气质还存在其他亲缘：都嗜好"sport"与赌博， relish aimless emulation，并且常常迷信运气、咒语、占卜与征兆。当环境有利时，这种迷信会表达为"servile devotional fervor"与对宗教仪式的谨守。作者认为，在这一点上，罪犯气质与金钱/ leisure class 气质的共同点，大于它与工业劳动者或游手好闲者的共同点。

### 限制与边界

　　该比较是类型学意义上的，不意味着所有成功者都是罪犯。它强调金钱竞争制度对某种人格类型的选择性奖励。

### 原文摘录

> "The ideal pecuniary man is like the ideal delinquent in his unscrupulous conversion of goods and persons to his own ends, and in a callous disregard of the feelings and wishes of others and of the remoter effects of his actions."
> "The kinship of the two types of temperament is further shown in a proclivity to 'sport' and gambling, and a relish of aimless emulation."
> "Where circumstances are favorable, this proclivity is apt to express itself in a certain servile devotional fervor and a punctilious attention to devout observances."
