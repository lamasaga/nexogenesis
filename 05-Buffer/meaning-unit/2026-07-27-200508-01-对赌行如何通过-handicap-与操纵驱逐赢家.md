### 核心表达

　　当 bucket shop 无法用正常手段击败稳定赢家时，会采取提高保证金要求、加收溢价、甚至在交易所操纵股价等手段，制造"bucket-shop drive"来 wipe out 客户仓位。

### 依据与细节

　　作者以 Cosmopolitan Stock Brokerage Company 为例：先是要求他缴纳三点保证金并加收半点、一点、最终一点半溢价。具体机制是：买入价若为 90，ticket 上写 91⅛，股价涨一点二五后平仓仍亏损。此外， Cosmopolitan 还试图与纽约经纪人合谋操纵 Sugar 股价：作者当日做空 3500 股 Sugar，价格跌至 103 后停滞，他因感觉不对劲而强行平仓；随后 telegraph 机狂响，报价被拉到 108，其他空头被 wipe out。作者称这种先急跌再瞬间回升为 "bucket-shop drive"。他还提到一位纽约交易所会员曾反向利用此机制，从全国 bucket shop 卷走七万美元。

### 限制与边界

　　原文为回忆录叙事，未提供操纵事件的独立验证；描述基于作者个人经历与当时市场惯例。

### 原文摘录

> They made me put up a three-point margin and compelled me to pay a premium at first of a half point, then a point, and finally, a point and a half.
> If the price was 90 when I bought, instead of making my ticket: "Bot Steel at 90⅛," it read: "Bot Steel at 91⅛." Why, that stock could advance a point and a quarter after I bought it and I’d still be losing money.
> In the old days whenever a bucket shop found itself loaded with too many bulls on a certain stock it was a common practice to get some broker to wash down the price of that particular stock far enough to wipe out all the customers that were long of it.
> Whenever there was an unexplained sharp drop which was followed by instant recovery, the newspapers in those days used to call it a bucket-shop drive.
