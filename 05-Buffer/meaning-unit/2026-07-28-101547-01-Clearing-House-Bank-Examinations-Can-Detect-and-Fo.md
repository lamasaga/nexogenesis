### 核心表达

Most bank failures result from the gradual accumulation of undesirable assets over years. Independent clearing-house bank examinations, if equipped with authority to recommend and enforce remedial action, can compel early liquidation of such assets and avert failures. Examination is not, however, a substitute for management control; it cannot prevent errors of judgment or dishonesty.

### 依据与细节

**Chicago system (inaugurated 1 June 1906):**
- Covers all associated banks and non-member institutions.
- Conducted with five regular assistants, each assigned to a specialized part of the work.
- Examines assets and liabilities and investigates the workings of every department.
- Produces a detailed duplicate report: one filed in the clearing-house vault, one handed to the examined bank's president for directors' use.
- Directors are individually notified that the examination has occurred and that a report is available; the examiner insists on acknowledged receipt.
- A special brief report in letter form is read to the clearing-house committee, describing asset character, loans to officers/directors/employees or their corporations, excessive or important loans, unwarranted conditions, irregularities, and dangerous tendencies.
- The detailed report is not submitted to the committee unless unusual conditions require it.

**Minneapolis system:**
- Operates under substantially the same conditions as Chicago.
- Principal difference: the examiner delivers the original report in person to the board of directors at a convened meeting, reads it, discusses criticisms, and considers recommendations. No director can claim lack of opportunity to review it.

### 限制与边界

Examinations are retrospective: "an event after the act" with no control over a bank's initiative. The examiner cannot judge every loan and is not responsible for management decisions. Effectiveness depends on examiner competence, director willingness to act, and the association's willingness to enforce recommendations. The text emphasizes examinations as restraint and disclosure, not as operational control.

### 原文摘录

> "Most bank failures are due to the gradual acquirement of undesirable assets over a period of years, and if some authority exists with power to make recommendations of a remedial character, with the further power to enforce such recommendations, if necessary, there is little doubt that many bank failures would be averted."

> "The clearing-house association of Chicago was the pioneer in the establishment of an independent system of clearing-house bank examinations in this country, its system having been inaugurated on June 1, 1906."

> "Examination is always an event after the act, having no control over a bank's initiative, which rests exclusively with the executive officers and directors."
