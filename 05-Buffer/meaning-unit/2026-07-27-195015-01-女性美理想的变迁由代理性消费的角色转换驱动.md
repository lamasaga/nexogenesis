### 核心表达

　　西方文化中理想女性体型的历史摆动——从健壮的荷马式少女，到骑士时代纤细虚弱的贵妇，再到现代回归自然体型——并非纯粹审美自主演变，而是由女性在各阶段作为劳动者、代理性闲暇者或独立消费者的社会经济角色决定的。

### 依据与细节

　　作者描述三个阶段。第一阶段，在掠夺文化中女性作为劳动奴隶被重视，理想是"robust, large-limbed woman"，如荷马史诗中的少女，"the ground of appreciation is the physique"。第二阶段，当上层妻子的职能变为纯粹代理性闲暇时，骑士浪漫理想强调面部精致、手脚纤细、腰肢极细，"the slender waist"甚至暗示 extreme debility，因为这证明她"incapable of useful effort and must therefore be supported in idleness by her owner"。第三阶段，现代工业高度发展使 leisure class 女性财富充裕到无需通过明显残疾来证明闲暇，"the ideal of feminine beauty is beginning to change back again... to a woman of the archaic type that does not disown her hands and feet"。作者总结："In the course of economic development the ideal of beauty among the peoples of the Western culture has shifted from the woman of physical presence to the lady, and it is beginning to shift back again to the woman; and all in obedience to the changing conditions of pecuniary emulation."

### 限制与边界

　　该模型未考虑医疗、营养、运动文化等独立因素对女性体型审美的影响。它将复杂审美变迁完全归因于经济角色与金钱竞争。

### 原文摘录

> "In the conventional scheme of those days ladies of high degree were conceived to be in perpetual tutelage, and to be scrupulously exempt from all useful work."
> "The resulting chivalric or romantic ideal of beauty takes cognizance chiefly of the face, and dwells on its delicacy, and on the delicacy of the hands and feet, the slender figure, and especially the slender waist."
> "In the course of economic development the ideal of beauty among the peoples of the Western culture has shifted from the woman of physical presence to the lady, and it is beginning to shift back again to the woman."
