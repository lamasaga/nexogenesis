### 核心表达

　　随着富裕 leisure class 规模扩大并形成内部舆论，服装中的支付能力展示从粗俗的" loud"形式转向更精细、更需要训练有素的眼光才能识别的符号。

### 依据与细节

　　作者描述，过去广告手段较为粗陋，因为受众包含大量未受训练的人。当足够大的富裕阶级形成后，成员之间有闲暇学习解读"subtler signs of expenditure"，"Loud" dress 反而变得可憎，因为它暴露了向 vulgar 展示的过度欲望。"To the individual of high breeding, it is only the more honorific esteem accorded by the cultivated sense of the members of his own high class that is of material consequence"。于是，上层 leisure class 倾向于将底层人口排除在观众之外，导致服装符号的"refinement of methods, a resort to subtler contrivances, and a spiritualization of the scheme of symbolism in dress"。由于上层阶级在所有体面事务上定调，整个社会也逐渐走向更精致的服装表达。这种对广告媒介的 finer discrimination 本身是"a very large element of the higher pecuniary culture"。

### 限制与边界

　　该现象描述的是 long-term 趋势，未提供具体历史节点或定量数据。它适用于解释 logomania 与 quiet luxury 之间的张力，但原文未预见品牌时代的符号机制。

### 原文摘录

> "The method of advertisement undergoes a refinement when a sufficiently large wealthy class has developed, who have the leisure for acquiring skill in interpreting the subtler signs of expenditure."
> "There arises a tendency to exclude the baser elements of the population from the scheme even as spectators whose applause or mortification should be sought."
> "As the community advances in wealth and culture, the ability to pay is put in evidence by means which require a progressively nicer discrimination in the beholder."
