### 核心表达

　　John Ruskin 与 William Morris 对手工艺与原始风格的推崇，在作者看来并非纯粹审美反动，而是对"荣誉性粗糙"的宣扬：这种风格要求更多手工劳动、使用不便且价格高昂，因而契合炫耀性浪费规范。

### 依据与细节

　　作者称，"Hence has arisen that exaltation of the defective, of which John Ruskin and William Morris were such eager spokesmen in their time"，其根基在于"the visibly more perfect goods were... the cheaper"（即机器产品）。Morris 晚年经营的 Kelmscott Press 被当作典型：其书籍在字体、纸张、插图、装订上都追求对古代粗糙工艺的近似，"require hand labor, are more expensive; they are also less convenient for use"。这些书籍因此"argue ability on the part of the purchaser to consume freely, as well as ability to waste time and effort"。例如，Kelmscott Press 出版现代使用的书籍，却采用"obsolete spelling, printed in black-letter, and bound in limp vellum fitted with thongs"，从纯功能角度看已近荒谬。限量发行 further 成为稀缺与昂贵的保证，"A limited edition is in effect a guarantee... that this book is scarce and that it therefore is costly and lends pecuniary distinction to its consumer"。作者特别声明，他仅从经济价值角度刻画这一倾向，并非贬低其美学贡献。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论 Morris 本人的社会主义政治意图或 Ruskin 的社会批评维度。该解释将手工艺运动还原为消费经济中的声誉机制。

### 原文摘录

> "Hence has arisen that exaltation of the defective, of which John Ruskin and William Morris were such eager spokesmen in their time."
> "The Kelmscott Press reduced the matter to an absurdity--as seen from the point of view of brute serviceability alone--by issuing books for modern use, edited with the obsolete spelling, printed in black-letter, and bound in limp vellum fitted with thongs."
> "A limited edition is in effect a guarantee--somewhat crude, it is true--that this book is scarce and that it therefore is costly and lends pecuniary distinction to its consumer."
