### 核心表达

　　现代文明住宅与公共建筑若剥离其荣誉性浪费元素，往往只剩下相对的不冒犯性；真正具有审美价值的部分常常是被艺术家忽视的侧墙与背面。

### 依据与细节

　　作者认为，物品若要同时满足金钱声誉与审美需求，必须通过"novelty"、"curiosity"、费解的设计等替代性品味来折中。于是许多所谓美的物品展现"considerable ingenuity of design"，同时给出"expenditure of labor in excess of what would give them their fullest efficiency"。这种 selective adaptation 在建筑中尤其有效："It would be extremely difficult to find a modern civilized residence or public building which can claim anything better than relative inoffensiveness in the eyes of anyone who will dissociate the elements of beauty from those of honorific waste"。城市高级公寓的正面呈现"endless variety of architectural distress and of suggestions of expensive discomfort"；而作为审美对象，"the dead walls of the sides and back of these structures, left untouched by the hands of the artist, are commonly the best feature of the building"。

### 限制与边界

　　该论断具有强烈主观性，未提供建筑实例或专家评估。它可被视为作者对维多利亚时代过度装饰建筑的一种批判性观察。

### 原文摘录

> "It would be extremely difficult to find a modern civilized residence or public building which can claim anything better than relative inoffensiveness in the eyes of anyone who will dissociate the elements of beauty from those of honorific waste."
> "The endless variety of fronts presented by the better class of tenements and apartment houses in our cities is an endless variety of architectural distress and of suggestions of expensive discomfort."
> "Considered as objects of beauty, the dead walls of the sides and back of these structures, left untouched by the hands of the artist, are commonly the best feature of the building."
