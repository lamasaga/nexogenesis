### 核心表达

　　Canadian banks carry reserves whose size is determined by each bank manager's daily judgment, based on the amount and character of liabilities, rather than by a uniform legal reserve requirement.

### 依据与细节

　　- The law does not prescribe a reserve ratio; it only requires that the bank satisfy depositor demands according to contract and redeem its notes on demand.
　　- The manager estimates needed reserve by considering time deposits versus demand deposits, the probable future needs of depositors, constant communication with branch managers, and many years of institutional experience.
　　- Public custom expects a bank to do a little more than the law requires, because its credit would suffer if it took advantage of legal privileges to delay payment on time deposits.
　　- The argument is that a hired manager, sworn to protect the bank's credit and trained in its service, will fix the reserve nearer the ideal than lawmakers or outsiders could.

### 原文摘录

> To the Canadian banker the word "reserve" means a fund immediately available for the liquidation of liabilities. How much this fund ought to be depends altogether upon the amount and character of the liabilities to be protected.
> The law neither helps nor hinders him; it simply requires that the bank shall satisfy the demands of depositors in accordance with the terms of the contract and that it shall redeem its notes on demand.
> Under these circumstances it would be remarkable if he did not fix the amount of his bank's reserve nearer the ideal figure--if an ideal banking reserve is possible--than could possibly be done by a body of lawmakers or of any other men outside the bank.
