### 核心表达

　　体育运动在气质上本质上是男孩式的，带有大量 make-believe 与戏剧性表演；这种幼稚性标志着参与者道德发展的某种停滞。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，"the temperament which inclines men to them is essentially a boyish temperament"。体育中的 make-believe 与儿童游戏相似：猎人携带过量武器以"impress upon their own imagination the seriousness of their undertaking"；他们采用"histrionic, prancing gait"并夸张潜行或攻击动作。竞技体育中几乎总有"rant and swagger and ostensible mystification"。体育行话也大量借用战争术语，"the slang of athletics... is in great part made up of extremely sanguinary locutions borrowed from the terminology of warfare"。作者认为，特殊行话的使用通常证明该职业"substantially make-believe"。

### 限制与边界

　　作者将成人体育还原为儿童游戏，忽略了体育中的真实身体挑战、技能精进与社群凝聚功能。该解释对仪式化、等级化的竞技体育更有说服力。

### 原文摘录

> "The addiction to sports, therefore, in a peculiar degree marks an arrested development of the man's moral nature."
> "This peculiar boyishness of temperament in sporting men immediately becomes apparent when attention is directed to the large element of make-believe that is present in all sporting activity."
> "Except where it is adopted as a necessary means of secret communication, the use of a special slang in any employment is probably to be accepted as evidence that the occupation in question is substantially make-believe."
