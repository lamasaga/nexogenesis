### 核心表达

　　成功的投机者必须保持身体与精神健康；亏损应源于判断错误，而非纵欲或过度放纵。规律作息与清醒头脑是持续交易的必要条件。

### 依据与细节

　　作者描述自己从上午十点到下午三点专注于击败市场，三点后享受生活。但他强调从不让享乐干扰生意。"When I lost it was because I was wrong and not because I was suffering from dissipation or excesses." 他从不熬夜，年轻时也不守夜，因为睡眠不足无法做好生意。他通常十点上床。他认为自己正在取得一种专业上的冷静态度，这种态度来自对自己谋生方式的超然。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论个体差异或极端市场压力下的作息调整；其纪律基于个人习惯。

### 原文摘录

> The game of beating the market exclusively interested me from ten to three every day, and after three, the game of living my life. Don’t misunderstand me. I never allowed pleasure to interfere with business.
> When I lost it was because I was wrong and not because I was suffering from dissipation or excesses. There never were any shattered nerves or rum-shaken limbs to spoil my game.
> Even now I am usually in bed by ten. As a young man I never kept late hours, because I could not do business properly on insufficient sleep.
