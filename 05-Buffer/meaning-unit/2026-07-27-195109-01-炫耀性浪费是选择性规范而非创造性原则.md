### 核心表达

　　炫耀性浪费本身并不直接创造新的消费习俗或艺术风格；它只作为选择性过滤器，使那些符合其浪费要求的创新得以存续。它是一种 regulative（调控性）而非 creative（创造性）的原则。

### 依据与细节

　　作者明确指出："Neither in matters of art and taste proper, nor as regards the current sense of the serviceability of goods, does this canon act as a principle of innovation or initiative. It does not go into the future as a creative principle which makes innovations and adds new items of consumption and new elements of cost." 相反，"The principle in question is, in a certain sense, a negative rather than a positive law. It is a regulative rather than a creative principle." 它很少直接发起习俗，而是对其他原因产生的习俗进行选择。"Conspicuous wastefulness does not directly afford ground for variation and growth, but conformity to its requirements is a condition to the survival of such innovations as may be made on other grounds." 在其他条件相同的情况下，"the more obviously wasteful usage or method stands the better chance of survival"。作者最后以圣经式语言总结："Its office is to prove all things and to hold fast that which is good for its purpose."

### 限制与边界

　　该模型区分了习俗的起源与存续，但未说明哪些非经济力量是创新的真正来源。它主要用于反驳将一切消费形式都直接归因于金钱攀比的简化论。

### 原文摘录

> "It is a regulative rather than a creative principle. It very rarely initiates or originates any usage or custom directly."
> "Other thing being equal, the more obviously wasteful usage or method stands the better chance of survival under this law."
> "The law of conspicuous waste does not account for the origin of variations, but only for the persistence of such forms as are fit to survive under its dominance."
