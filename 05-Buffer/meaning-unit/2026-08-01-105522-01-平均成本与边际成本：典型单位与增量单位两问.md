Caleb 做产量决策时须区分两问：「典型一杯咖啡成本多少？」与「多产 1 杯额外花多少？」前者用平均，后者用边际，答案并不相同。**Average total cost（ATC）** = total cost ÷ quantity；因 TC = FC + VC，故 ATC 可写为 **average fixed cost（AFC）** 与 **average variable cost（AVC）** 之和：AFC = FC/Q，AVC = VC/Q。例：每小时 2 杯时 TC = $3.80，ATC = $3.80/2 = **$1.90/杯**。

> Average total cost tells us the cost of a typical unit of output if total cost is divided evenly over all the units produced.
