### 核心表达

　　炫耀性闲暇与炫耀性消费本质相同：二者都是浪费，前者浪费时间与精力，后者浪费物品。在社会眼中，它们被接受为展示财富的等价手段，选择哪一种只是广告效率问题。

### 依据与细节

　　作者总结："the utility of both alike for the purposes of reputability lies in the element of waste that is common to both"。闲暇是"waste of time and effort"，消费是"waste of goods"。两者都是"methods of demonstrating the possession of wealth"，并被习俗接受为"equivalents"。因此，"The choice between them is a question of advertising expediency simply"，除非受到其他礼仪标准的干扰。作者指出，在社会规模较小、个人声誉依赖邻里口碑时，两种方法效果相当；但当社会分化加深、需要影响更广泛的人群时，消费开始占据优势，因为陌生人的唯一判断依据是可见的商品展示。

### 限制与边界

　　原文承认，实际发展中并非只有效率逻辑，还有 workmanship 本能、宗教与社交动机等其他力量在调节炫耀性浪费。因此该等价模型是作者为了突出共同结构而做的抽象。

### 原文摘录

> "In the one case it is a waste of time and effort, in the other it is a waste of goods. Both are methods of demonstrating the possession of wealth, and the two are conventionally accepted as equivalents."
> "The choice between them is a question of advertising expediency simply, except so far as it may be affected by other standards of propriety, springing from a different source."
> "So long as the community or social group is small enough... so long the one method is about as effective as the other."
