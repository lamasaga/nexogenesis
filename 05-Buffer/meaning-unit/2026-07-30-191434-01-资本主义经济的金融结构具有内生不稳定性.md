### 核心表达

　　资本主义经济——尤其当其拥有复杂、精密且不断演化的金融结构时——会内生地趋向“不连贯”（incoherence），即失控通胀或深度萧条；但这种不连贯不必完全实现，因为制度与政策可以抑制不稳定趋势，实现“稳定不稳定”。

### 依据与细节

- 作者把经济不稳定归因于经济的**内部过程**，而非外部冲击或政策制定者的无知无能。
- 战后金融关系与机构的累积变化造成**脆弱的金融体系**；经济成功运行本身诱发了**投机性融资**，使基于1950-60年代经验的货币财政政策规则失效。
- 书中援引复杂系统研究的证据：几乎所有多维、非线性、时间依赖的系统都是**内生不稳定**的（Richard Day、Alessandro Vercelli、Peter Albin 等）；若不稳定系统受到上限与下限约束，计量分析会误判其为稳定系统。
- 资本主义若要持续存在，不能长期在资产价值崩溃/失业威胁与加速通胀/猖獗投机威胁之间剧烈摆动。

### 限制与边界

- 原文未提及：作者没有在本段给出“脆弱性”的可操作量化指标，也未给出预测下一次危机时点的工具。
- 原文未提及：关于复杂系统非线性稳定的引证是旁证性文献回顾，未建立与宏观金融数据的具体对应。

### 原文摘录

> "The economic instability so evident since the late 1960s is the result of the fragile financial system that emerged from cumulative changes in financial relations and institutions over the years following World War II. The unintended and often unnoticed changes in financial relations, and the speculative finance induced by the successful functioning of the economy, have made the rules for monetary and fiscal policy based on the experience of the 1950s and the early 1960s invalid."

> "The major flaw of our type of economy is that it is unstable. This instability is not due to external shocks or to the incompetence or ignorance of policy makers. Instability is due to the internal processes of our type of economy. The dynamics of a capitalist economy which has complex, sophisticated, and evolving financial structures leads to the development of conditions conducive to incoherence—to runaway inflations or deep depressions."

> "There is now ample evidence to indicate that almost all systems which are multidimensional, nonlinear, and time dependent are endogenously unstable."
