### 核心表达

　　拟人化宗教的核心是一种 status 关系：人作为下级，面对具有上级人格的超自然力量。这种关系巩固了从掠夺文化中继承来的等级与顺从习惯。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，"an anthropomorphic cult is a code of detailed relations of status superimposed upon the concept of a preternatural, inscrutable propensity in material things"。这种 cult 可以被视为"an outgrowth of archaic man's pervading animistic sense, defined and in some degree transformed by the predatory habit of life"。结果是"a personified preternatural agency, which is by imputation endowed with a full complement of the habits of thought that characterize the man of the predatory culture"。作者总结，status 关系、掠夺习惯和拟人化崇拜三者之间存在"mutual causal relation"：status 是 invidious comparison 的正式表达；拟人化崇拜则在这种比较中建立人与神之间的上下级关系。因此，"the emulative habit of life and the habit of devout observances"是野蛮人性和现代野蛮变体的互补元素。

### 限制与边界

　　该解释将宗教还原为社会结构与心理习惯的表达，未讨论宗教体验、教义内容或灵性需求的独立作用。它不适用于所有宗教传统，尤其是那些强调非人格终极实在的宗教。

### 原文摘录

> "An anthropomorphic cult, in the days of its early vigor at least, is an institution the characteristic element of which is a relation of status between the human subject as inferior and the personified preternatural agency as superior."
> "The cult may be taken as an outgrowth of archaic man's pervading animistic sense, defined and in some degree transformed by the predatory habit of life."
> "The emulative habit of life and the habit of devout observances are therefore to be taken as complementary elements of the barbarian type of human nature."
