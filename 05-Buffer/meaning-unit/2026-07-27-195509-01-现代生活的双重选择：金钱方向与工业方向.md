### 核心表达

　　现代社会的选择压力并非单一，而是沿着两条分歧线路展开：一条是金钱/攀比方向，保存 barbarian temperament；另一条是工业/非攀比方向，培养对机械效率与人际善意的非个人兴趣。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，"The development in other respects runs on two divergent lines"。就能力保存而言，可称为 pecuniary 与 industrial；就倾向与精神态度而言，可称为 invidious/self-regarding 与 non-invidious/economical；就认知倾向而言，前者是个人立场、qualitative relation、status、worth，后者是 impersonal standpoint、sequence、quantitative relation、mechanical efficiency、use。金钱职业主要调用并保存前者，工业职业主要锻炼并保存后者。由于个体具有统一性，这两组能力、态度与兴趣分别构成人性的两种替代方向。金钱生活一般保存 barbarian temperament，但以 fraud and prudence 或 administrative ability 取代早期野蛮人的 physical damage 偏好。不过这种替代并不完全，因为体面的消费生活仍要求 exercise 某些早期 barbarian traits。

### 限制与边界

　　该二分法高度抽象，现实中个人往往同时参与金钱与工业活动。原文也承认这一区分"by no means a hard and fast distinction between classes of persons"。

### 原文摘录

> "As regards the conservation of propensities, spiritual attitude, or animus, the two may be called the invidious or self-regarding and the non-invidious or economical."
> "The pecuniary employments call into action chiefly the former of these two ranges of aptitudes and propensities, and act selectively to conserve them in the population."
> "The tendency of the pecuniary life is, in a general way, to conserve the barbarian temperament, but with the substitution of fraud and prudence, or administrative ability, in place of that predilection for physical damage that characterizes the early barbarian."
