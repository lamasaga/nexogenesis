### 核心表达

　　Exchange Banks 的核心业务是在印度贴现以伦敦付款的贸易汇票，同时尽量在伦敦吸收存款；由于票款在伦敦收取而采购汇票的资金需在印度垫付，它们不断通过购买 Council Bills（或 telegraphic transfers）、sovereigns 甚至银块来平衡两地头寸。因此，Council Bills 的需求主要由 Exchange Banks 在印度的贴现业务量决定。

### 依据与细节

　　- Exchange Banks 因 Presidency Banks 被禁止从事外汇业务和海外融资而兴起。
　　- 真正重要的 Exchange Banks 有六家：Delhi and London Bank（1844）、Chartered Bank of India, Australia, and China（1853）、National Bank of India（1863）、Hong Kong and Shanghai Banking Corporation（1864）、Mercantile Bank of India（1893）、Eastern Bank（1910）。
　　- 它们从印度和伦敦吸收存款，力求在伦敦多吸存；定期存款一年以上付 4 或 3½ percent，活期按最低月余额或超额部分付 2 percent。
　　- 大部分资金用于购买/贴现汇票：在印度买入、以伦敦为付款地的汇票占多数；因这些汇票可在伦敦再贴现，Bank 得以周转。
　　- 头寸平衡：当在印度买入汇票时，银行需把伦敦资金调往印度；方式包括购买 Council Bills/TT、从英格兰银行或埃及/澳洲银行代理处买 sovereigns、偶尔运银块。
　　- 若 Secretary of State 取消 TT 便利或迫使银行运金到印度，会增加印度出口商将货物变现的延迟与成本；若印度季节差、出口减少，汇票供给减少，对 Council Bills 的需求也相应下降。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未披露各 Exchange Bank 在印度与伦敦的具体资产负债拆分；所描述的是理想型商业模式，个别银行可能有大量非印度业务（如 Chartered、Hong Kong Bank）。

### 原文摘录

> As the majority of the bills are bought by the Banks in India, while cash is received for them, either at maturity or through rediscount, in London, the Banks are constantly in the position of finding themselves in funds in London and of wishing to have funds (for the purchase of more bills) in India.
> They proceed, therefore, to even up their accounts as between London and India by buying, in London, Council Bills (or transfers) or sovereigns ... for delivery in India.
> If he withdraws the convenience of telegraphic transfers or forces the Banks to put themselves in funds in India by sending sovereigns, he causes delay or additional expense in the discounting of bills in India.
