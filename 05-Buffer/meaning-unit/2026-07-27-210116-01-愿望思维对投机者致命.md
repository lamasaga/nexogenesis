### 核心表达

　　华尔街的大人物与政治家、普通 sucker 一样容易陷入愿望思维；但对投机者而言，这种态度是致命的。证券制造商或企业发起人或许可以沉溺于希望，但投机者不能。

### 依据与细节

　　作者在 Aix 读到 Smelters 增加股息的消息后，认为这意味着牛市集团仍在绝望地对抗基本条件、常识与诚信——他们知道将要发生什么，于是通过提高股息来推高市场，以便在风暴前出货。作者说："The big men of the Street are as prone to be wishful thinkers as the politicians or the plain suckers. I myself can’t work that way. In a speculator such an attitude is fatal. Perhaps a manufacturer of securities or a promoter of new enterprises can afford to indulge in hope-jags." 他立即知道唯一舒适的做法是做空 Smelters，因为内幕人士几乎是在恳求他做空。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论内幕人士可能 genuinely 看好前景的情况；其判断基于对当时熊市的信念。

### 原文摘录

> The big men of the Street are as prone to be wishful thinkers as the politicians or the plain suckers. I myself can’t work that way. In a speculator such an attitude is fatal.
> At all events, I knew that all bull manipulation was foredoomed to failure in that bear market. The instant I read the dispatch I knew there was only one thing to do to be comfortable, and that was to sell Smelters short.
