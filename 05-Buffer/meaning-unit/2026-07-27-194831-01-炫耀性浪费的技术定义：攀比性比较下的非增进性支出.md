### 核心表达

　　作者将"浪费"用作中性技术术语：凡是主要源于 invidious pecuniary comparison（攀比性金钱比较）、从整体人类福祉角度看并不直接增进生活的支出，即属炫耀性浪费。消费者个人可能已将其视为必需品，但这不改变其技术分类。

### 依据与细节

　　作者强调，判断浪费的试金石不是消费者个人是否感到满足，而是"whether it serves directly to enhance human life on the whole--whether it furthers the life process taken impersonally"。这一标准来自 workmanship 本能，是"the court of final appeal in any question of economic truth or adequacy"。凡可归因于"the habit of making an invidious pecuniary comparison"的习俗性支出，都应归入浪费。作者列举地毯、挂毯、银餐具、侍者服务、丝质高帽、浆洗过的亚麻布、珠宝服饰等为例，说明它们起初主要 wasteful，后来却被消费者视为生活必需。同时，作者拒绝纯粹二分法：大多数物品同时具有 useful 与 wasteful 两种成分，"An article may be useful and wasteful both, and its utility to the consumer may be made up of use and waste in the most varying proportions"。消费性物品通常浪费成分更大，生产性物品则相反。

### 限制与边界

　　作者明确说明，该术语"not to be taken in an odious sense"，不谴责消费者个人选择。但技术定义与日常贬义之间的张力本身也被作者视为 workmanship 本能的社会显现。

### 原文摘录

> "The test to which all expenditure must be brought in an attempt to decide that point is the question whether it serves directly to enhance human life on the whole."
> "An article may be useful and wasteful both, and its utility to the consumer may be made up of use and waste in the most varying proportions."
> "It is here called 'waste' because this expenditure does not serve human life or human well-being on the whole, not because it is waste or misdirection of effort or expenditure as viewed from the standpoint of the individual consumer who chooses it."
