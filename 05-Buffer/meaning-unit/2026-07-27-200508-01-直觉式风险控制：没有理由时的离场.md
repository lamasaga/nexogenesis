### 核心表达

　　当交易者感到"不对劲"却无法指出具体原因时，最佳策略是离场观望；没有明确理由的直觉不安，往往是对隐藏危险的早期信号，盲目坚持比承认不确定更危险。

### 依据与细节

　　作者在 Sugar 交易中做空 3500 股，价格按预期下跌后停顿。按照他的 figuring，价格应继续跌破 103，但他反而更不安。他说："I don’t do things blindly. I don’t like to. I never did. Even as a kid I had to know why I should do certain things. But this time I had no definite reason to give to myself, and yet I was so uncomfortable that I couldn’t stand it." 他让 Dave Wyman 替他报价格，自己走到柜台前；听到电报机异常声响后，他在 Dave 报出"Su--"的瞬间把单子拍在柜台上大喊"Close Sugar!"，以 103 平仓。随后价格被拉到 108，他因此逃过一劫。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未说明这种直觉是否可靠到可系统化为规则；叙事中这次成功可能带有后见之明。

### 原文摘录

> I knew something was wrong somewhere, but I couldn’t spot it exactly. But if something was coming and I didn’t know where from, I couldn’t be on my guard against it. That being the case I’d better be out of the market.
> I don’t do things blindly. I don’t like to. I never did. Even as a kid I had to know why I should do certain things. But this time I had no definite reason to give to myself, and yet I was so uncomfortable that I couldn’t stand it.
> Just then Dave Wyman by the ticker, began: "Su--" and quick as a flash I slapped my tickets on the counter in front of the clerk and yelled, "Close Sugar!" before Dave had finished calling the price.
