### 核心表达

　　John Jay Knox 在1885年2月波士顿商人俱乐部演讲中猛烈批评共和、民主两党在银币问题上只说“陈词滥调”，主张格罗弗·克利夫兰政府应借拨款法案之机废除有利于银矿业的铸币法，并称这将提振工业、改善国会代表素质。

### 依据与细节

　　- Knox 认为1884年大选中“银币问题本应是 burning issue”，但两党纲领对此“nothing but platitudes”。
- 他批评共和党候选人在接受信中回避问题，民主党候选人则“said nothing”。
- 他援引 Edward Atkinson 的权威，称全美银产量“not equal... to the production of eggs by the hens of this country”，以此贬低自由铸银的经济合理性。
- 他声称克利夫兰真心希望停止银币铸造，“nothing would please him more than to have a clause inserted in an appropriation bill which would repeal the law which was passed in the interest of silver miners”。
- 他将公务员改革与停止银币铸造并列为克利夫兰政府若敢推动就能“lift the depression which now burdens every industrial interest”的两大改革。
- 他强调执行改革“will require more bottom than brains”。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提及：Knox 演讲的听众反应、Atkinson 数据的具体来源、以及克利夫兰是否真有此意。本片段立场鲜明，属于 Knox 个人观点，不应等同于客观分析。

### 原文摘录

> "The platforms of both parties in the late campaign contained nothing but platitudes upon the silver question, which should have been the burning issue."
> "I am told by good authority that Governor Cleveland is earnest in his desire to stop the coinage, and that nothing would please him more than to have a clause inserted in an appropriation bill which would repeal the law which was passed in the interest of silver miners..."
> "It will require some intellect to work out these reforms. But it will require more bottom than brains..."
