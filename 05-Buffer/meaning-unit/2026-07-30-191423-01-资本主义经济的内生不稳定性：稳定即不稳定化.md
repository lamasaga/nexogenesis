### 核心表达

　　Minsky 认为，现代资本主义经济的内在动力本身就是不稳定的；一段时期的相对安宁（**periods of tranquility**）会改变行为，鼓励冒险与创新，从而瓦解产生安宁的条件，即"**稳定即不稳定化**"（stability is destabilizing）。经济至多是"**条件性协调**"（conditionally coherent），而非趋向均衡。

### 依据与细节

- 安宁时期金融结构稳健、创新稀少，投资中的金融因素不那么重要；但市场力量会在稳定时推动系统走向不稳定。
- 经济行为人会减少安全边际（margins of safety），转向更投机性的融资结构。
- 这一过程是**内生的**（endogenous），不是外部冲击或"非理性繁荣"的结果。
- 引述 Keynes 的话："在不稳定的经济中，投机支配企业"（"in an unstable economy speculation dominates enterprise"）。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提及：未给出定量指标来界定"稳定期"的长度；未详细说明不同国家制度环境下该机制的差异。

### 原文摘录

> "stability is destabilizing," as relative tranquility encourages more risk-taking and innovative behavior that increases income even as it disrupts the conditions that generate "coherency" and "tranquility."
> "the market forces that operate when a system is stable will push it toward instability, so that even if anything like an equilibrium could be achieved, it would set off behavioral responses that would quickly move the economy away from equilibrium."
> "Unlike other analyses that blame 'shocks,' 'irrational exuberance,' or 'foolish' policy, Minsky argued that the processes that generate financial fragility are 'natural,' or endogenous to the system."
