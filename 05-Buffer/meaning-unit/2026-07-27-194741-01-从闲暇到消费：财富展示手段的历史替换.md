### 核心表达

　　在准和平文化阶段，炫耀性闲暇是财富与体面的首要标志；进入和平工业阶段后，随着人际环境扩大与奴隶制消失，炫耀性消费逐渐取代闲暇成为主导手段，但并未完全吞噬生产剩余。

### 依据与细节

　　作者描述了一个历史替换过程。起初 leisure 占据首位，"during the quasi-peaceable culture"其地位远高于浪费性消费。原因在于古代 noble/ignoble 职业区分：闲暇显示免除卑贱劳动，具有荣誉性；在小型稳定社会中，它同样是财富的有力证据。然而，这种机制导致"a large impecunious leisure class"，甚至限制工业生产至生存最低水平。奴隶制下的强制劳动才使产出超过生存最低线。随着社会规模扩大与匿名观察增加，consumption 作为广告手段的效率上升；同时 workmanship 本能也逐渐削弱对明显浪费的认同。因此，"consumption has gained ground, until, at present, it unquestionably holds the primacy"，但"it is still far from absorbing the entire margin of production above the subsistence minimum"。

### 限制与边界

　　该模型是高度概括的历史分期，未提供具体年代或跨文化证据。作者明确承认 workmanship 本能、宗教传统等其他因素也在调节这一过程。

### 原文摘录

> "Leisure held the first place at the start, and came to hold a rank very much above wasteful consumption of goods, both as a direct exponent of wealth and as an element in the standard of decency, during the quasi-peaceable culture."
> "From that point onward, consumption has gained ground, until, at present, it unquestionably holds the primacy, though it is still far from absorbing the entire margin of production above the subsistence minimum."
> "The early ascendency of leisure as a means of reputability is traceable to the archaic distinction between noble and ignoble employments."
