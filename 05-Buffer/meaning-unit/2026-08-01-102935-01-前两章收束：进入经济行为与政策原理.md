本书前两章已介绍 **ideas and methods of economics**，现 **ready to get to work**——下一章起更详学习 **principles of economic behavior and policy**。阅读全书需调动多种 intellectual skills；Keynes 提醒：经济学研究 **does not seem to require any specialized gifts of an unusually high order**，相对哲学或纯科学似 **very easy subject**，却 **at which very few excel**——悖论或在于 master-economist 须 rare **combination** of gifts。
