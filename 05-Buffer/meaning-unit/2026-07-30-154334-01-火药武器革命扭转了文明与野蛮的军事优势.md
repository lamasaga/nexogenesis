### 核心表达

　　**火药及火器**的发明使战争耗费剧增，能负担这笔费用的富裕文明国获得对贫穷野蛮国的明显优势；在古代，富裕文明国难以抵御贫穷野蛮国，现代则相反。

### 依据与细节

- 火器使武器和弹药更昂贵：一支滑膛枪比标枪或弓箭贵；一门大炮或迫击炮比弩炮昂贵且沉重。
- 现代演习消耗的粉末不可回收，花费巨大；古代标枪、弓箭可轻易回收且价值甚微。
- 现代炮兵优势使城镇防御成本大增，难以短期抵御攻击。
- 因此，现代战争中富裕文明国对贫穷野蛮国具有明显优势；火药发明虽看似有害，却有利于文明的持久与扩展。

### 原文摘录

> "The great change introduced into the art of war by the invention of fire-arms, has enhanced still further both the expense of exercising and disciplining any particular number of soldiers in time of peace, and that of employing them in time of war."

> "In modern war, the great expense of firearms gives an evident advantage to the nation which can best afford that expense; and, consequently, to an opulent and civilized, over a poor and barbarous nation."

> "The invention of fire-arms, an invention which at first sight appears to be so pernicious, is certainly favourable, both to the permanency and to the extension of civilization."
