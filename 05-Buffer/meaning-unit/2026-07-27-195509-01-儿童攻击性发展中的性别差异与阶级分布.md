### 核心表达

　　男孩在成长过程中通常经历一个明显的掠夺性阶段，表现为好斗、自我孤立、恶作剧；女孩则较少或较轻微地经历这一阶段。这种儿童期的掠夺气质在 leisure class 与罪犯阶级中常持续至成年，而在工业大众中则被抑制。

### 依据与细节

　　作者观察到，婴幼儿时期攻击性较低，"The child's temperament does not commonly answer to this description during infancy and the years of close tutelage"。男孩随后进入 predaceous interval，"mischievousness of the boy"出现；女孩则"seldom accomplished with the same degree of completeness"，且"less accentuated"。作者认为，儿童期暂时重现了早期野蛮文化的气质，而成人气质相当于"spiritual maturity"。在那些精神发展未达平均水平的人中，这种儿童式好斗气质会持续到成年，"remain as an undissolved residue of crude humanity"。作者推测，这种 bellicose temperament 在某种程度上是 race characteristic，在 dominant upper-class ethnic type（dolicho-blond）中比在 subservient lower-class types 中更显著。

### 限制与边界

　　原文承认该概括需要"very material qualification"，且基于对"race characteristic"的假设。现代发展心理学会提供更多关于社会化、性别角色、环境刺激的解释。

### 原文摘录

> "In the male child the predaceous interval is ordinarily fairly well marked and lasts for some time."
> "In girls the transition to the predaceous stage is seldom accomplished with the same degree of completeness as in boys."
> "The traits that distinguish the swaggering delinquent and the punctilious gentleman of leisure from the common crowd are, in some measure, marks of an arrested spiritual development."
