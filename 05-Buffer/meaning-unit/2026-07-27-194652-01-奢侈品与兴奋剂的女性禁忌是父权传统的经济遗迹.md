### 核心表达

　　女性对酒精、麻醉剂等兴奋剂保持更高节制，部分原因是父权制下"女性作为财产"观念的延续：女性应当只为自身生存或主人的舒适与声誉而消费，奢侈品消费本身属于主人的特权。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，酒精与麻醉品若价格高昂，便被感知为 noble and honorific。因此下层阶级尤其是女性，对其实行"enforced continence"。从古代到父权制时代，女性负责制备并供应这些奢侈品，而消费特权属于出身高贵的男性。醉酒及其病理后果反而成为荣誉标志，"a mark, at the second remove, of the superior status of those who are able to afford the indulgence"。作者进一步论证，女性更大节制并非完全出于生理或健康理由，而是"in some part due to an imperative conventionality"；这种习俗性约束在父权传统最强的地区最强。传统认为女性作为 chattel（财产），应只消费维持生存所必需之物，除非其消费有助于主人的舒适或声誉。即使在现代西方中产阶级中，"the women are to the greatest extent subject to a qualified tabu on narcotics and alcoholic beverages"。

### 限制与边界

　　作者承认存在诸多限定条件，随着父权传统弱化，禁忌也逐渐松弛。此外，作者也预见到女性服饰与家庭摆设似乎是明显例外，但暗示这一例外在后续章节会被重新解释。

### 原文摘录

> "If these articles of consumption are costly, they are felt to be noble and honorific. Therefore the base classes, primarily the women, practice an enforced continence with respect to these stimulants."
> "The woman, being a chattel, should consume only what is necessary to her sustenance,--except so far as her further consumption contributes to the comfort or the good repute of her master."
> "The use of these various stimulants is obnoxious to at least one, if not both, of these objections; and it is a fact too significant to be passed over that it is precisely among these middle classes of the Germanic culture... that the women are to the greatest extent subject to a qualified tabu on narcotics and alcoholic beverages."
