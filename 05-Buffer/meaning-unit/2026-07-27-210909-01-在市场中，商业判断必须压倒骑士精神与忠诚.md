### 核心表达

　　股票市场不讲骑士精神，也不奖励忠诚；投机者的本职是永远支持自己的判断，任何让他违背自身判断的考虑——即便出于感激、礼貌或道德义务——都是不智的。

### 依据与细节

　　作者反思自己在 Williamson 办公室的行为：作为一个体面人，他的做法无可厚非；但作为一个投机者，这是 improper and unwise。他引用 “Noblesse oblige--but not in the stock market, because the tape is not chivalrous and moreover does not reward loyalty”。他认为自己无法因此改变人格，但 Business is business，投机者的业务就是始终 backing his own judgment。

### 限制与边界

　　作者承认他无法为了交易而“重塑自己”，说明这是人格与职业角色之间的张力，而非简单的意志力问题。

### 原文摘录

> It was proper for me as a man to act the way I did in Dan Williamson’s office, but it was improper and unwise for me as a speculator to allow myself to be influenced by any consideration to act against my own judgment.
> Noblesse oblige--but not in the stock market, because the tape is not chivalrous and moreover does not reward loyalty.
> my business as a speculator is to back my own judgment always.
