### 核心表达

　　截至1885年，美国铸币局（以费城为主）雇佣约300名职员与工人，其中约三分之二为男性、三分之一为女性，雇员数量随工作量波动。

### 依据与细节

　　- 脚注 [8] 原文："For the various duties of the Mint there are over three hundred persons employed as clerks, workmen, etc.—say about two-thirds men and one-third women—the number depending, of course, upon the amount of work to be done."
- 这表明19世纪末铸币局已是一个中等规模的工业-行政复合机构，且女性劳动力在文员或调整部门占显著比例。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提供具体岗位分布、薪酬性别差异、或各分支铸币局的雇员数据。

### 原文摘录

> "For the various duties of the Mint there are over three hundred persons employed as clerks, workmen, etc.—say about two-thirds men and one-third women—the number depending, of course, upon the amount of work to be done."
