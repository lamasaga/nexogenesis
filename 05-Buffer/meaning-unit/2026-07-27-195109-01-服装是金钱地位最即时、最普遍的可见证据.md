### 核心表达

　　在所有消费形式中，服装是最适合展示金钱地位的媒介，因为它始终处于他人视线之中，能在第一眼就传递支付能力信息。

### 依据与细节

　　作者在第7章指出，"no line of consumption affords a more apt illustration than expenditure on dress"。服装的优势在于："our apparel is always in evidence and affords an indication of our pecuniary standing to all observers at the first glance"。在所有阶级中，服装支出的很大一部分"is incurred for the sake of a respectable appearance rather than for the protection of the person"。人们对服装 shabby 的感觉比对其他消费更为敏锐，"probably at no other point is the sense of shabbiness so keenly felt"。为维持体面，人们甚至愿意在严寒气候中"go ill clad in order to appear well dressed"。服装的商业价值"is made up to a much larger extent of the fashionableness, the reputability of the goods than of the mechanical service which they render"。因此，服装需要是一种"higher"或精神性需要。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论制服、工装或宗教服饰等具有非金钱象征功能的服装类型。该主张针对的是日常社会着装。

### 原文摘录

> "Our apparel is always in evidence and affords an indication of our pecuniary standing to all observers at the first glance."
> "No one finds difficulty in assenting to the commonplace that the greater part of the expenditure incurred by all classes for apparel is incurred for the sake of a respectable appearance rather than for the protection of the person."
> "It is by no means an uncommon occurrence, in an inclement climate, for people to go ill clad in order to appear well dressed."
