### 核心表达

　　在现代工业社会，机械装置已足以满足日常舒适，普通家庭之所以仍雇用仆人，主要是出于"pecuniary decency"（金钱体面）的传统规范，而非真实的舒适需求。仆人的存在是将一部分繁琐的体面消费外包出去。

### 依据与细节

　　作者观察到，现代家庭中机械设备已高度发达，"body servants... would now scarcely be employed by anybody except on the ground of a canon of reputability carried over by tradition from earlier usage"。唯一真正例外的，是照顾病患与衰弱者的训练有素护士，但这不属于 domestic servants 范畴。作者进一步分析，所谓"无法胜任家务"的理由可还原为两点：一是家庭成员被迫参与社交义务（calls, drives, clubs, sewing-circles, sports, charity organisations），以表演"conspicuous leisure"；二是体面消费要求下，住所、家具、衣物、餐饮等 apparatus 变得繁复笨重，消费者无法独自按规范处理。因此，与雇工的直接接触虽令人不快，却因其承担了"onerous consumption of household goods"而被忍受。仆人本质上是"a concession of physical comfort to the moral need of pecuniary decency"。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论低收入家庭或近期零工经济（如外卖、保洁平台）对仆役功能的替代。该主张适用于作者所处时代的"moderately well-to-do household"，即中等以上家庭。

### 原文摘录

> "In the modern industrial communities the mechanical contrivances available for the comfort and convenience of everyday life are highly developed. So much so that body servants, or, indeed, domestic servants of any kind, would now scarcely be employed by anybody except on the ground of a canon of reputability carried over by tradition from earlier usage."
> "The proximate reason for keeping domestic servants... is (ostensibly) that the members of the household are unable without discomfort to compass the work required by such a modern establishment."
> "The presence of domestic servants... is a concession of physical comfort to the moral need of pecuniary decency."
