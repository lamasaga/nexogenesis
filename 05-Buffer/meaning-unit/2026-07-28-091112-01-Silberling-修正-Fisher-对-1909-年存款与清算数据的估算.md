### 核心表达

　　Silberling 为安德森核查了 1909 年 3 月 17 日纽约以外 12 个主要城市的清算数据，发现当日实际清算额与该年平均日清算额之比仅为 1.0245:1。据此修正后，全国存款总额应约为 11.1 亿美元（日）/3330 亿美元（年），远低于费雪估算的 12 亿/3640 亿。

### 依据与细节

　　- 调查的 12 个城市占纽约以外全国清算额的 69%：Chicago、Philadelphia、Boston、St. Louis、Pittsburg、San Francisco、Baltimore、New Orleans、Atlanta、Providence、St. Paul、Seattle；
　　- 1909 年这些城市平均日清算额 $136,222,436；3 月 17 日实际清算额 $132,961,273；比率 1.0245:1；
　　- Silberling 为未调查的 31%  outside clearings 留出 3% 的保守下调；
　　- 按费雪方法，纽约存款乘 1.28，纽约以外存款乘 1.03，得到日存款 11.1 billions；年费雪原值为 12 billions→364 billions；修正为 11.1 billions→333 billions。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提及：费雪是否承认该修正；该修正对交易方程 MV+M'V'=PT 的总体影响。

### 原文摘录

> "The daily average of clearings for these cities in 1909 was $136,222,436; the actual clearings for March 17, 1909, was $132,961,273. The ratio of average daily clearings to actual clearings on March 17 was 1.0245:1."
> "...he multiplies the deposits assigned by Professor Fisher to New York by 1.28, and the deposits assigned to the country outside by 1.03, getting total deposits for the day of 1.11 billions, as against Professor Fisher's figure of 1.20 billions, and a total for the year of 333 billions, as against a total obtained by Professor Fisher of 364 billions."
