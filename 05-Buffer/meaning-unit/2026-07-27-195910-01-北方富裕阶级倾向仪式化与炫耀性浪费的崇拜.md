### 核心表达

　　美国北方富裕阶层虽然没有深厚的宗教传统，但倾向于在婚礼、葬礼等荣誉性场合采用宗教仪式，并偏好强调仪式与壮观附属品的教派；这既可能是真正的虔诚返祖，也可能是为了符合体面规范的保护性模仿。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，北方富裕阶级"of too recent growth to be possessed of a well-formed transmitted habit"，但"there is a perceptible tendency among this class to give in at least a nominal, and apparently something of a real, adherence to some one of the accredited creeds"。婚礼、葬礼等 honorific events "pretty uniformly solemnized with some especial degree of religious circumstance"。作者无法判断这种依附在多大程度上是"bona fide reversion"，多大程度上是"protective mimicry assumed for the purpose of an outward assimilation to canons of reputability borrowed from foreign ideals"。但他观察到上层信徒倾向于加入"those cults which lay relatively great stress on ceremonial and on the spectacular accessories of worship"；在上层会员占主导的教会中，存在"a tendency to accentuate the ritualistic, at the cost of the intellectual features in the service"。这种仪式化可能部分源于对"conspicuously wasteful spectacles"的偏好，也可能部分反映虔诚的 archaic 形式。

### 限制与边界

　　作者承认无法区分真正虔诚与社会模仿。该观察针对的是特定历史时期的美国上层宗教生活，不能推广到所有富裕社会。

### 原文摘录

> "Weddings, funerals, and the like honorific events among this class are pretty uniformly solemnized with some especial degree of religious circumstance."
> "There is a tendency perceptible among the upper-class worshippers to affiliate themselves with those cults which lay relatively great stress on ceremonial and on the spectacular accessories of worship."
> "This peculiar development of the ritualistic element is no doubt due in part to a predilection for conspicuously wasteful spectacles."
