### 核心表达

　　大学中的运动员往往同时是虔诚的宗教实践者；宗教组织也利用体育比赛作为皈依手段与维持信徒虔诚态度的工具，因为二者调动的是同一范围的心理倾向。

### 依据与细节

　　作者引用校园生活中广为人知的现象："the desirable athletic material afforded by any student body... is at the same time predominantly religious; or... given to devout observances to a greater degree than the average of those students whose interest in athletics... is less"。大学运动员常投身宗教宣传，且倾向于传播"more anthropomorphic cults"，强调人与拟人化神祇之间的个人 status 关系。宗教团体如 YMCA、Christian Endeavor 等致力于"practical religion"，并"commonly devote some appreciable portion of their energies to the furtherance of athletic contests and similar games of chance and skill"。作者甚至说，"sports of this kind are apprehended to have some efficacy as a means of grace"，因为它们锻炼 animistic sense 与 emulative propensity，有助于形成和保存虔诚心态。

### 限制与边界

　　该观察基于作者时代的美国大学文化，未必适用于所有国家或所有时期的大学。它解释了某些校园现象，但不能概括所有运动员或所有宗教组织。

### 原文摘录

> "The desirable athletic material afforded by any student body in this country is at the same time predominantly religious."
> "These lay religious bodies commonly devote some appreciable portion of their energies to the furtherance of athletic contests and similar games of chance and skill."
> "The games which give exercise to the animistic sense and to the emulative propensity help to form and to conserve that habit of mind to which the more exoteric cults are congenial."
