### 核心表达

　　现代机械工业要求工人持续面对非个人的、定量的因果序列；这种日常训练瓦解了将现象解释为个人意志或超自然干预的泛灵论习惯，从而导致不信倾向。

### 依据与细节

　　作者对比了手工业与现代工业：在手工业中，"the workman is himself the prime mover chiefly depended upon"，自然力量在很大程度上被视为不可控的；因此用个人动机解释现象的习惯不会受到持续事实的严重冲击。但在现代工业中，"the prime movers and the contrivances through which they work are of an impersonal, non-individual character"，工人日常面对"matter-of-fact sequence"。结果是"a proclivity to undevout scepticism"。机械工人的岗位越来越成为"discretion and supervision in a process of mechanical, dispassionate sequences"。因此，"the animistic interpretation of phenomena and that respect of persons on which devout observance proceeds are in process of obsolescence" among those habitually in immediate contact with modern industrial processes。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论宗教如何在工业化社会中以新形式（如基要主义、灵恩运动）复兴。该线性世俗化假设已被20世纪宗教社会学部分修正。

### 原文摘录

> "Under the later developed industrial processes... the grounds of generalization habitually present in the workman's mind... is an enforced cognizance of matter-of-fact sequence."
> "The result, so far as concerns the workman's life of faith, is a proclivity to undevout scepticism."
> "Familiarity with the highly organized and highly impersonal industrial processes of the present acts to derange the animistic habits of thought."
