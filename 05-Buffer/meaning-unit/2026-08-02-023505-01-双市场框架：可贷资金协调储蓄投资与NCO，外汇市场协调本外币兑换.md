理解开放经济的作用力，先分别看两个市场。可贷资金市场协调国民储蓄、国内投资以及流向国外的可贷资金（净资本流出 NCO）。外汇市场协调本币与外币的兑换需求。本节先各自刻画供求，下一节再合成为开放经济的整体均衡。

> The first is the market for loanable funds, which coordinates national saving, domestic investment, and the flow of loanable funds to be invested abroad (called the net capital outflow). The second is the market for foreign-currency exchange, which coordinates people who want to trade domestic and foreign monies.
