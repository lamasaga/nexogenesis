### 核心表达

　　1907–8 年危机中印度内部货币市场出奇宽松，Bank of Bengal 利率最高仅 6 percent，远低於 Bank of England 的 7 percent。但这是一种误导性的特例：歉收使金融家手中积压大量无法运用的 rupee 资金，银行无需为流动性发愁。未来若伴随国内银行危机，情况会截然不同。

### 依据与细节

　　- 1907 年 11 月 Bank of England rate 达 7 percent 时，Bank of Bengal rate 未超过 6 percent。
　　- 当时无银行挤兑迹象，也无显著 hoarding 回流；正在学习使用银行的人群没有恐慌。
　　- 原因：作物歉收使金融家持有大量闲置 rupee 资金，银行能轻易获得 rupee/notes；政府问题只是把本地货币转为 sterling。
　　- 但此后印度 Joint Stock Banking 以“not perfectly sound lines”大规模扩张，下次危机很可能伴随银行困难。
　　- 欧洲（法、德、奥）在巴尔干战争期间的 hoarding 复兴说明：只要传统尚未远去，危险信号一现，囤钱习惯会迅速复活。

### 限制与边界

　　原文对“not perfectly sound lines”的指责未给出具体银行数据或倒闭案例；对欧洲 hoarding 的比较是类比，未证明印度必然同样严重。

### 原文摘录

> During the eventful weeks in November 1907, when the Bank of England rate stood at 7 per cent, the Bank of Bengal rate did not rise above 6 per cent.
> The easiness of the internal money market at that time and the total absence of banking trouble have produced the impression that there will be plenty of rupee funds available at a crisis, and that the only question will be as to whether the Government can turn these into sterling.
> The great development of Indian Joint Stock Banking since that time on not perfectly sound lines makes it doubtful whether bank troubles will be absent in an equal degree on the next occasion of difficulty.
