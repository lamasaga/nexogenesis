### 核心表达

　　良心、诚实、公平感与 workmanship 本能等品质，并非源于掠夺或金钱文化，而是人类在更早的和平野蛮阶段形成的古老遗存；这些品质在竞争制度下受到压抑，但从未完全消失。

### 依据与细节

　　作者认为，这些和平特质"could scarcely have arisen and acquired fixity during"从掠夺文化至今的 status 与阶级对立时期；它们更可能来自更早的和平生活方法，并在掠夺与准和平文化中处于"incipient, or at least imminent, desuetude"状态。这些特质包括"instinct of race solidarity which we call conscience, including the sense of truthfulness and equity, and the instinct of workmanship, in its naive, non-invidious expression"。它们作为遗传特征持续存在，因为"rest on a broad basis of race continuity"。即使在掠夺文化的严重选择下，这些和平特质也未被完全消除。作者承认，和平野蛮人在经济效率上也有弱点：weakness, inefficiency, lack of initiative and ingenuity, yielding and indolent amiability；但他们也具备 truthfulness, peaceableness, good-will 等有利于集体生活的品质。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提供考古或心理学证据证明存在一个统一的"和平野蛮阶段"。该主张是演化社会学推测，而非实证结论。

### 原文摘录

> "Among these archaic traits that are to be regarded as survivals from the peaceable cultural phase, are that instinct of race solidarity which we call conscience, including the sense of truthfulness and equity, and the instinct of workmanship, in its naive, non-invidious expression."
> "These peaceable traits are in great part alien to the methods and the animus of barbarian life."
> "It is entirely probable that these traits have come down from an earlier method of life, and have survived through the interval of predatory and quasi-peaceable culture."
