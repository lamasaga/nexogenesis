### 核心表达

　　束腰与裹小脚等身体畸形之所以被视为美，并非出于自然审美，而是因为它们证明女性无需劳动、由所有者供养，从而成为金钱实力的可见证据。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，当炫耀性闲暇被视为获得声誉的重要手段时，理想女性体型要求"delicate and diminutive hands and feet and a slender waist"。这些特征"go to show that the person so affected is incapable of useful effort and must therefore be supported in idleness by her owner"。她无用且昂贵，因此"valuable as evidence of pecuniary strength"。女性会主动改造身体以符合时代教化品味，而男性在金钱体面规范引导下，会觉得这些人为造成的病态特征有吸引力。作者明确将西方束腰与中国缠足并列："the constricted waist which has had so wide and persistent a vogue in the communities of the Western culture, and so also the deformed foot of the Chinese"。二者对未训练的感觉来说都是"unquestioned repulsiveness"，需要 habituation 才能接受，但一旦纳入金钱声誉体系，便成为"pecuniary and cultural beauty"，并被当作女性气质理想的元素。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论这些习俗内部的性别能动性、医疗后果或反抗运动。该解释强调经济功能的审美转化，是一种结构功能主义解读。

### 原文摘录

> "These features... go to show that the person so affected is incapable of useful effort and must therefore be supported in idleness by her owner."
> "Both of these are mutilations of unquestioned repulsiveness to the untrained sense. It requires habituation to become reconciled to them."
> "They are items of pecuniary and cultural beauty which have come to do duty as elements of the ideal of womanliness."
