### 核心表达

　　投机者必须相信自己的判断；依赖他人的内幕消息或建议无法赚大钱，因为卖出时机到来时消息提供者可能不在场，而交易者自己却缺乏独立决策能力。

### 依据与细节

　　作者说："A man must believe in himself and his judgment if he expects to make a living at this game." 他举 Smith 的 tip 为例：若按 Smith 的 tip 买入，就必须按 Smith 的 tip 卖出，这等于依赖他人。"Suppose Smith is away on a holiday when the selling time comes around?" 他从经验中知道，没有人能通过别人给的一系列 tip 比自己判断赚更多钱。他花了五年时间才学会足够聪明地玩游戏，以便在判断正确时赚大钱。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论信息优势、合法研究或网络价值；其立场基于个人经验中的"tip"文化。

### 原文摘录

> A man must believe in himself and his judgment if he expects to make a living at this game. That is why I don’t believe in tips.
> If I buy stocks on Smith’s tip I must sell those same stocks on Smith’s tip. I am depending on him. Suppose Smith is away on a holiday when the selling time comes around?
> I know from experience that nobody can give me a tip or a series of tips that will make more money for me than my own judgment. It took me five years to learn to play the game intelligently enough to make big money when I was right.
