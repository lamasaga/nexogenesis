### 核心表达

　　在 leisure class 规范下，任何不能归类为炫耀性闲暇的活动都被排除在生活方案之外；体育因同时具备明显的无用性与表面的目的性，成为唯一获得体面充分认可的户外活动形式。

### 依据与细节

　　作者解释，猎人、钓鱼者常以热爱自然、需要 recreation 为动机，但这些需求本可以"more readily and fully satisfied without the accompaniment of a systematic effort to take the life of those creatures"。真正原因在于：由掠夺性 leisure class 建立的礼仪规范"will not permit him, without blame, to seek contact with nature on other terms"。体育从掠夺文化继承而来，"the only form of outdoor activity that has the full sanction of decorum"。体面准则排除所有不能归类为炫耀性闲暇的活动；同时，无目的的体力活动又令人厌烦。体育满足了两个要求："substantial futility together with a colorable make-believe of purpose"，并提供 emulation 的空间。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未考虑园艺、徒步、观鸟等非杀戮性户外活动在作者时代是否被接受。该主张将体育的垄断性体面地位归因于阶级规范。

### 原文摘录

> "From being an honorable employment handed down from the predatory culture as the highest form of everyday leisure, sports have come to be the only form of outdoor activity that has the full sanction of decorum."
> "Sports satisfy these requirements of substantial futility together with a colorable make-believe of purpose."
> "In order to be decorous, an employment must conform to the leisure-class canon of reputable waste; at the same time all activity... must conform to the generically human canon of efficiency for some serviceable objective end."
