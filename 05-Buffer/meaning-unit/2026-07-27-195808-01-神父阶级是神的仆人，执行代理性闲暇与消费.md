### 核心表达

　　神父阶级在经济功能上是神的贴身仆人：他们必须避免从事世俗生产劳动，其消费也应是代理性消费而非增进自身舒适；在现代教派中，这种生活仍被视为真正教士礼仪的核心。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，除了向俗人征收代理性闲暇贡品外，还有专门的祭司阶层"whose time is wholly set apart for a similar service"。祭司不仅必须戒绝"vulgar labor, especially so far as it is lucrative or is apprehended to contribute to the temporal well-being of mankind"，而且即使可以不求助于工业而获得世俗利益，也被禁止追求，"it is felt to be unworthy of the servant of the divinity... that he should seek material gain or take thought for temporal matters"。神父不应从事机械生产劳动，但应大量消费；其消费须符合代理性消费规则，不显然增进自身舒适。许多精致教派甚至要求"mortification of the flesh"。现代最新教派中，教士若表现出"levity and avowed zest in the enjoyment of the good things of this world"，也会被认为不符合真正教士礼仪。祭司阶层"rank high in the social scale by virtue of this borrowed light"，其消费是 vicarious consumption，其职业是 vicarious leisure in the full sense。

### 限制与边界

　　该模型主要针对具有祭司制度与繁文缛节的宗教传统。作者承认现代某些教派已偏离此方案，将其视为"half-caste priesthood"或处于转变中的祭司阶层。

### 原文摘录

> "It is not only incumbent on the priestly class to abstain from vulgar labor... The tabu in the case of the priestly class goes farther and adds a refinement in the form of an injunction against their seeking worldly gain."
> "Their consumption is vicarious consumption; and since, in the advanced cults, their master has no need of material gain, their occupation is vicarious leisure in the full sense."
> "Whatever suggests that these servants of an invisible master are living a life, not of devotion to their master's good fame, but of application to their own ends, jars harshly on our sensibilities."
