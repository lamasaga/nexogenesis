### 核心表达

　　在人类经济动机中，emulation（攀比）可能仅次于自我保存本能。在现代工业社会中，它表现为金钱攀比，并通过炫耀性浪费吸收社会生产效率提高所带来的任何剩余。

### 依据与细节

　　作者断言："With the exception of the instinct of self-preservation, the propensity for emulation is probably the strongest and most alert and persistent of the economic motives proper"。在工业社会中，这种攀比转化为 pecuniary emulation，"virtually equivalent to... conspicuous waste"。因此，"The need of conspicuous waste... stands ready to absorb any increase in the community's industrial efficiency or output of goods, after the most elementary physical wants have been provided for"。作者引用 J. S. Mill 的话："hitherto it is questionable if all the mechanical inventions yet made have lightened the day's toil of any human being"，以说明技术进步带来的剩余并未转化为闲暇，而是被更高的炫耀性消费标准吞噬。结果是，"the energies of the industrious members of the community are bent to the compassing of a higher result in conspicuous expenditure, rather than slackened to a more comfortable pace"。

### 限制与边界

　　作者提到两种例外：财富增长过快使消费习惯跟不上；或个体推迟消费以追求更壮观的总体效果。这两种情况并未否定基本机制，而是其时间形态上的变体。

### 原文摘录

> "With the exception of the instinct of self-preservation, the propensity for emulation is probably the strongest and most alert and persistent of the economic motives proper."
> "The need of conspicuous waste, therefore, stands ready to absorb any increase in the community's industrial efficiency or output of goods, after the most elementary physical wants have been provided for."
> "The strain is not lightened as industrial efficiency increases and makes a lighter strain possible, but the increment of output is turned to use to meet this want, which is indefinitely expansible."
