### 核心表达

　　具有体育/赌博气质者与下层罪犯阶级，比普通社区成员更可能信奉某种被认可的信条并遵守虔诚仪式；他们若原本不信仰，也更容易改宗。其所倾向的通常是具有彻底拟人化神祇的教派。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，"Both the delinquent and the sporting man are on the average more apt to be adherents of some accredited creed, and are also rather more inclined to devout observances, than the general average of the community"。不信仰者若来自这些阶级，也"show more of a proclivity to become proselytes to some accredited faith than the average of unbelievers"。这一现象甚至被体育辩护者当作优点来强调。然而，这些人所依附的 cult 通常"not one of the so-called higher faiths, but a cult which has to do with a thoroughly anthropomorphic divinity"。作者解释，"Archaic, predatory human nature is not satisfied with abstruse conceptions of a dissolving personality"，如一神论哲学中的第一因、宇宙智慧、世界灵魂等。他以救世军（Salvation Army）为例：其成员部分来自下层罪犯，军官中也有不成比例的前运动员。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提供统计证据，仅基于观察。该主张对强调个人关系与情感皈依的福音派教派解释力较强，对仪式复杂或神学抽象的宗教传统解释力较弱。

### 原文摘录

> "Both the delinquent and the sporting man are on the average more apt to be adherents of some accredited creed, and are also rather more inclined to devout observances, than the general average of the community."
> "The cult to which sporting men and the predaceous delinquent classes adhere... is ordinarily not one of the so-called higher faiths, but a cult which has to do with a thoroughly anthropomorphic divinity."
> "Archaic, predatory human nature is not satisfied with abstruse conceptions of a dissolving personality that shades off into the concept of quantitative causal sequence."
