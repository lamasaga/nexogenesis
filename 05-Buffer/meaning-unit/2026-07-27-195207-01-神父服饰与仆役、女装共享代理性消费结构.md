### 核心表达

　　神父服饰在华丽、不便、苦行等特征上与仆役制服和女装相似，因为神父在经济功能上是神的贴身仆人，其消费与闲暇都是为神明代理进行的。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，除了仆役，"the priestly class"的服饰也呈现出女性服饰的特征："Priestly vestments show, in accentuated form, all the features that have been shown to be evidence of a servile status and a vicarious life"。祭服 ornate, grotesque, inconvenient，"comfortless to the point of distress"。神父被期待不从事有用劳动，在公共场合保持"impassively disconsolate countenance"，酷似训练有素的家仆。剃须面也是同一效果的补充。其经济原因在于："the priest is a body servant, constructively in attendance upon the person of the divinity whose livery he wears"。他的 livery 昂贵以彰显神的尊严，但又必须显示穿戴者未从中获得身体舒适，因为消费声誉归于不在场的神主，而非仆人自己。

### 限制与边界

　　该比较可能不适用于所有宗教传统，尤其是无圣职人员或反仪式倾向的教派。作者以基督教和拟人化宗教为主要参照。

### 原文摘录

> "The priest is at the same time expected to refrain from useful effort and, when before the public eye, to present an impassively disconsolate countenance, very much after the manner of a well-trained domestic servant."
> "In economic theory, the priest is a body servant, constructively in attendance upon the person of the divinity whose livery he wears."
> "It is contrived to show that the wearing of it contributes little or nothing to the physical comfort of the wearer, for it is an item of vicarious consumption."
