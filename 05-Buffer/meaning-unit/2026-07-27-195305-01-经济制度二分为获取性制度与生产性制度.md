### 核心表达

　　经济制度可依据功能分为两类：服务于 invidious 利益的获取性（pecuniary/business）制度，与服务非攀比性利益的生产性（industrial）制度。leisure class 主要与前一类相关，其利益在于财产的保有与转移。

### 依据与细节

　　作者借用古典术语，将经济制度分为"institutions of acquisition or of production"，或用 earlier terms 说是"pecuniary or industrial institutions"，或"serving either the invidious or the non-invidious economic interest"。前者涉及"business"，后者涉及 industry 的机械性意义。作者指出，工业制度常被忽视，因为它们不直接涉及统治阶级，因此很少成为立法或 deliberate convention 的主题。leisure class 对经济过程的关系是"pecuniary relation--a relation of acquisition, not of production; of exploitation, not of serviceability"。其功能是"of a parasitic character"，利益在于"divert what substance they may to their own use, and to retain whatever is under their hand"。商业世界的惯例在 predation or parasitism 的选择性监督下成长起来，是所有权惯例，源于古代掠夺文化。

### 限制与边界

　　作者声明并非贬低 propertied class 的经济功能，只是指出其关系的性质。该二分法是其制度经济学的基础，但当代经济学可能用不同术语表述。

### 原文摘录

> "They are institutions of acquisition or of production... they are pecuniary or industrial institutions... they are institutions serving either the invidious or the non-invidious economic interest."
> "The relation of the leisure (that is, propertied non-industrial) class to the economic process is a pecuniary relation--a relation of acquisition, not of production; of exploitation, not of serviceability."
> "Their office is of a parasitic character, and their interest is to divert what substance they may to their own use, and to retain whatever is under their hand."
