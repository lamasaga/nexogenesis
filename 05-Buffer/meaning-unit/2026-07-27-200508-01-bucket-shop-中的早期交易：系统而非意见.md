### 核心表达

　　作者在 bucket shop（对赌行）中并非依据内幕消息或个股偏好交易，而是依据一套基于数字和过去价格形态的算术系统；这种操作方式与 bucket shop 的本质——赌报价带上的波动——高度契合。

### 依据与细节

　　作者十四岁时用一本小册子记录观察与预测，重点不是模拟交易盈亏，而是验证自己是否观察准确。他与同事凑钱第一次买入 Burlington，不是想翻倍，而是想测试自己的 figuring。后来在午休时间自行交易，"playing a system and not a favorite stock or backing opinions"。bucket shop 的合法经营中，波动本身就足以消灭小额保证金； author's ideal way was to bet on fluctuations as printed by the ticker.

### 限制与边界

　　原文未深入分析 bucket shop 的商业模式对市场的系统性影响；叙事集中于个人经历。

### 原文摘录

> I got so interested in my game and so anxious to anticipate advances and declines in all the active stocks that I got a little book. I put down my observations in it. It was rather a sort of record of my hits and misses.
> I was playing a system and not a favorite stock or backing opinions. All I knew was the arithmetic of it.
> As a matter of fact, mine was the ideal way to operate in a bucket shop, where all that a trader does is to bet on fluctuations as they are printed by the ticker on the tape.
