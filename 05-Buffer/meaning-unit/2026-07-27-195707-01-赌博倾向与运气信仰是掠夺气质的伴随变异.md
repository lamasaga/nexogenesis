### 核心表达

　　赌博倾向是野蛮气质的伴随变异，其核心是运气信仰。运气信仰本身比掠夺文化更古老，但在掠夺纪律下被塑造成赌博习惯的特定形式。

### 依据与细节

　　第11章指出，"The gambling propensity is another subsidiary trait of the barbarian temperament"，在运动员和从事战争、竞争性活动的人中普遍存在。它"recognized to be a hindrance to the highest industrial efficiency"。运气信仰"doubtfully to be classed as a feature belonging exclusively to the predatory type"，其元素可追溯至 predatory culture 之前的阶段。它可能属于"a stage in human evolution antedating the predatory culture"，是"one form of the artistic apprehension of things"。但作者认为，"it may well have been under the predatory culture that the belief in luck was developed into the form in which it is present... as the chief element of the gambling proclivity"。因此，作为赌博习惯的运气信仰是"an archaic trait, inherited from a more or less remote past, more or less incompatible with the requirements of the modern industrial process"。

### 限制与边界

　　作者区分了作为古老心理习惯的运气信仰与作为赌博习惯的运气信仰，但未提供二者演化的具体历史证据。该主张依赖演化心理学框架。

### 原文摘录

> "The gambling propensity is another subsidiary trait of the barbarian temperament."
> "The belief in luck is in substance a habit of more ancient date than the predatory culture. It is one form of the artistic apprehension of things."
> "In so far as the belief in luck comes to expression in the form of laying a wager, it is to be accounted an integral element of the predatory type of character."
