### 核心表达

　　宗教仪式中的建筑、服饰、祭品、节日服装等消费，以及祭司教育、服务、朝圣、斋戒等个人服务消费，本质上是炫耀性浪费；它们服务于神的声誉，并巩固 status 关系所要求的思维模式。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，"The consumption of ceremonial paraphernalia required by any cult... serves no immediate material end"，因此"without implying deprecation, be broadly characterized as items of conspicuous waste"。同样，priestly education, priestly service, pilgrimages, fasts, holidays, household devotions 等个人服务消费也属此类。这些仪式的执行"serve to extend and protract the vogue of those habits of thought on which an anthropomorphic cult rests"，即 regime of status 的思维习惯。因此，"the consumption of goods and effort in the service of an anthropomorphic divinity means a lowering of the vitality of the community"。作者还比较了为神服务与为野蛮时代酋长/贵族服务的消费之间的相似性：二者都需要专用昂贵建筑、特殊华丽服饰、礼拜者比日常更昂贵的衣着。

### 限制与边界

　　作者明确排除宗教消费的道德、审美与间接影响，只讨论其直接经济后果。该主张不评判信仰真假或仪式精神价值。

### 原文摘录

> "The consumption of ceremonial paraphernalia required by any cult... serves no immediate material end. All this material apparatus may... be broadly characterized as items of conspicuous waste."
> "They further the habits of thought characteristic of the regime of status."
> "The consumption of goods and effort in the service of an anthropomorphic divinity means a lowering of the vitality of the community."
