### 核心表达

　　在下层中产阶级家庭中，丈夫往往已没有真正闲暇，但仍拼命工作以让妻子能够按社会规范表演代理性闲暇与消费。妻子的"体面"家务因此是丈夫支付能力与社会声誉的间接证据。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，在中产阶级家庭中，"the head of the household has been reduced by economic circumstances to turn his hand to gaining a livelihood"，其本人已无法展示"conspicuous leisure of the master"。但"the derivative fact--the vicarious leisure and consumption rendered by the wife... remains in vogue as a conventionality which the demands of reputability will not suffer to be slighted"。常见景象是：男人勤勉工作，"in order that his wife may in due form render for him that degree of vicarious leisure which the common sense of the time demands"。妻子的闲暇并非真正懒惰，而是伪装成家务、社交礼仪等形式的"wasted effort"，其社会功能在于证明她"does not occupy herself with anything that is gainful or that is of substantial use"。即使家务产生某种审美效果，这些效果之所以被认可，也是因为品味已被"canon of propriety that demands just these evidences of wasted effort"所塑造。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论双职工家庭或女性独立收入对这一机制的影响。该模型以单收入、男主外女主内的中产阶级家庭为前提。

### 原文摘录

> "It is by no means an uncommon spectacle to find a man applying himself to work with the utmost assiduity, in order that his wife may in due form render for him that degree of vicarious leisure which the common sense of the time demands."
> "The leisure rendered by the wife in such cases is, of course, not a simple manifestation of idleness or indolence. It almost invariably occurs disguised under some form of work or household duties or social amenities."
> "The more reputable, 'presentable' portion of middle-class household paraphernalia are, on the one hand, items of conspicuous consumption, and on the other hand, apparatus for putting in evidence the vicarious leisure rendered by the housewife."
