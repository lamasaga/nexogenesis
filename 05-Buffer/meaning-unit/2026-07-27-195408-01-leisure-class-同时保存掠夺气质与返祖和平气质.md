### 核心表达

　　leisure class 一方面通过获取性职业保存掠夺气质，另一方面因其免受环境压力，为返祖至和平野蛮气质的个人提供了生存与传递特征的空间。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，leisure class 参与工业过程时，其训练倾向于"conserve in them the barbarian temperament"。但该阶级也因其庇护地位而有另一面："Individuals so placed as to be exempt from strain may survive and transmit their characteristics even if they differ widely from the average of the species"。由于 leisure class 在某种程度上被 shelter 于工业形势压力之外，"it should afford an exceptionally great proportion of reversions to the peaceable or savage temperament"。作者观察到上层阶级中有人投身慈善事业，并存在支持改革与改善的 sentiment；这些努力"bears the marks of that amiable 'cleverness' and incoherence that is characteristic of the primitive savage"。但作者也怀疑这些事实是否真正证明上层阶级中返祖比例更高，因为贫困阶级即使同样有这些倾向，也缺乏时间、精力与手段来表达。

### 限制与边界

　　该主张包含推测性：作者自己也不确定观察到的慈善行为是否证明返祖比例更高。它同时依赖对"primitive savage"气质的刻板描述。

### 原文摘录

> "The employments of the leisure classes in modern industry are such as to keep alive certain of the predatory habits and aptitudes."
> "The leisure class is in some degree sheltered from the stress of the industrial situation, and should, therefore, afford an exceptionally great proportion of reversions to the peaceable or savage temperament."
> "There is... an appreciable proportion of the upper classes whose inclinations lead them into philanthropic work, and there is a considerable body of sentiment in the class going to support efforts of reform and amelioration."
