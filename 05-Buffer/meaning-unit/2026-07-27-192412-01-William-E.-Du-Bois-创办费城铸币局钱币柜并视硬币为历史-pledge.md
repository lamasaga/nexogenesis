### 核心表达

　　William E. Du Bois 于1838年在费城铸币局创办钱币柜（Cabinet of coins），1846年出版《Pledges of History》描述该收藏；他认为硬币的价值在于其历史教益与艺术价值，而非单纯稀缺性。

### 依据与细节

　　- 1838年开始筹建钱币柜，每年从国会获得小额拨款，收藏工作完全交给 Du Bois。
- 他以冷静判断而非狂热逐稀的方式支出；部分最佳标本从铸币局存款中按金属价值挑选。
- 收藏成形并分类后，1846年出版描述，标题为“Pledges of History, etc.”。
- 标题暗示其观点：硬币应因其历史教导或艺术价值而被珍视， discourage “仅仅因为稀有而占有”的狂热。
- 他与 Jacob R. Eckfeldt 合著1842年《Manual of the Gold and Silver Coins of all nations, struck within the past century》，涵盖硬币、金银锭、伪币等主题；1850-1851年出补编。
- 他为美国哲学学会撰写多篇报告，包括《The Natural Dissemination of Gold》。
- 1869年在《Bankers' Magazine》发表《Propositions for a Revised System of Weights, and a Restoration of Silver Currency》。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未说明钱币柜的后续规模、Du Bois 个人收藏与官方收藏的界限、以及1869年银币恢复文章的具体论点。

### 原文摘录

> "He instituted the Cabinet of coins which now adorns the Mint. This was commenced in 1838. A small annual appropriation was procured from Congress for this purpose, and the work of collection committed entirely to Mr. Du Bois."
> "The title thus selected intimated his opinion as to the real value of such collections. He thought that a coin should be prized for its historical teaching, or artistic merit, and discouraged the rage to possess a piece simply because of its rarity."
> "In 1869 he wrote for the 'Bankers' Magazine,' 'Propositions for a Revised System of Weights, and a Restoration of Silver Currency.'"
