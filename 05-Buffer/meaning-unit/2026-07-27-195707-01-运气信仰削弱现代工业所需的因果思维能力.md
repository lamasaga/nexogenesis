### 核心表达

　　现代工业过程要求个体能够按定量因果关系理解和适应事实序列；运气信仰等泛灵论习惯会模糊因果理解，从而降低个人与集体的工业效率。

### 依据与细节

　　作者强调，现代工业的"intelligence"几乎就是"a degree of facility in the apprehension of and adaptation to a quantitatively determined causal sequence"。"In so far as the individual's inherited aptitudes or his training incline him to account for facts and sequences in other terms than those of causation or matter-of-fact, they lower his productive efficiency or industrial usefulness"。这种效率损失在大型工业中尤为明显，因为现代工业日益成为"comprehensive system of organs and functions mutually conditioning one another"，需要从业者无偏见地把握因果序列。在手工业中，灵巧、勤奋、体力尚可弥补思维偏见；但在发达的机械体系中，"the virtues of the handicraftsman count for less and less as an offset to scanty intelligence or a halting acceptance of the sequence of cause and effect"。即便是轻微的泛灵论倾向，"may work an appreciable lowering of the collective industrial efficiency of a community"。

### 限制与边界

　　作者将宗教、道德、审美层面的泛灵论价值排除在讨论之外，只关注其对工业效率的影响。该主张不评价信仰的真假或精神价值。

### 原文摘录

> "The 'intelligence' demanded of the workman... is little else than a degree of facility in the apprehension of and adaptation to a quantitatively determined causal sequence."
> "In so far as the individual's inherited aptitudes or his training incline him to account for facts and sequences in other terms than those of causation or matter-of-fact, they lower his productive efficiency or industrial usefulness."
> "Through its cumulative effect upon the habitual attitude of the population, even a slight or inconspicuous bias towards accounting for everyday facts by recourse to other ground than that of quantitative causation may work an appreciable lowering of the collective industrial efficiency of a community."
