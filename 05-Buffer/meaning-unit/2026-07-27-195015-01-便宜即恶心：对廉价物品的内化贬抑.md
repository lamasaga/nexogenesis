### 核心表达

　　人们最初因廉价暗示贫困而轻视节俭生活方式，最终却习惯于将"便宜"本身视为内在可耻。这种内化如此彻底，以至于"Cheap and nasty"成为不证自明的格言，即使在私密消费中也要避免廉价。

### 依据与细节

　　作者追溯了态度转变：起初人们不赞成廉价生活，因为"it indicated inability to spend much, and so indicated a lack of pecuniary success"。但代代传承之后，"they end by falling into the habit of disapproving cheap things as being intrinsically dishonorable or unworthy because they are cheap"。最终社会达到如此确信，"that we have no longer any misgivings in formulating the maxim, 'Cheap and nasty.'" 这种习惯根深蒂固，即使在没有展示意图的私密消费中，"we instinctively insist upon at least some measure of wasteful expensiveness"。作者以烛光晚餐为例：蜡烛曾是最便宜的家庭照明，如今却比油灯、煤气灯或电灯更受欢迎，因为"Candlelight is now softer, less distressing to well-bred eyes"，而三十年前并非如此。这说明物品的价值随其金钱声誉符号而改变。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论节俭运动、环保主义或极简主义等反文化潮流对该规范的挑战。该主张描述的是作者观察到的主流消费伦理。

### 原文摘录

> "While men may have set out with disapproving an inexpensive manner of living because it indicated inability to spend much... they end by falling into the habit of disapproving cheap things as being intrinsically dishonorable or unworthy because they are cheap."
> "So thoroughly has the habit of approving the expensive and disapproving the inexpensive been ingrained into our thinking that we instinctively insist upon at least some measure of wasteful expensiveness in all our consumption."
> "A political sage still living has summed up the conclusion of this whole matter in the dictum: 'A cheap coat makes a cheap man,' and there is probably no one who does not feel the convincing force of the maxim."
