### 核心表达

　　与经济制度二分相对应，职业也可分为获取性（pecuniary）与生产性（industrial）两类。前者通过教育和选择保存掠夺气质，后者则促进非攀比性、机械事实导向的思维习惯。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，现代经济制度分为 pecuniary 与 industrial；职业 likewise："Under the former head are employments that have to do with ownership or acquisition; under the latter head, those that have to do with workmanship or production"。leisure class 的经济利益在于 pecuniary employments，工人阶级则主要涉及 industrial。获取性职业的训练"acts to conserve and to cultivate certain of the predatory aptitudes and the predatory animus"，它既教育从事这些职业的个人，也选择性地压抑和淘汰不适应者。在和平的现代社会中，获取性职业主要培养"the peaceable range of predatory habits"，即 proficiency in fraud 而非 forcible seizure。相反，直接从事生产技术操作的人持续面对机械事实与序列，"the educative and selective action of the industrial process... acts to adapt their habits of thought to the non-invidious purposes of the collective life"，加速掠夺性向的过时。

### 限制与边界

　　作者承认这一区分并非绝对：工人也参与工资竞争、消费竞争等金钱活动。因此这是关于职业倾向而非个人品格的完全分类。

### 原文摘录

> "Under the former head are employments that have to do with ownership or acquisition; under the latter head, those that have to do with workmanship or production."

> "The educative and selective action of the industrial process... acts to adapt their habits of thought to the non-invidious purposes of the collective life."
