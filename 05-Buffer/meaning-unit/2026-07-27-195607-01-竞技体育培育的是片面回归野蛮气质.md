### 核心表达

　　以足球为代表的竞技体育，对体格与性格的塑造并非全面恢复野蛮状态，而是片面强化破坏性与欺诈性气质，同时压制野蛮人性格中对社会生活有益的方面。

### 依据与细节

　　作者讽刺地说，"the relation of football to physical culture is much the same as that of the bull-fight to agriculture"。为了这些 ludic 制度，需要对材料（人或动物）进行 careful selection and discipline，以"secure and accentuate certain aptitudes and propensities which are characteristic of the ferine state, and which tend to obsolescence under domestication"。结果不是全面恢复野蛮习性，而是"a one-sided return to barbarism... a rehabilitation and accentuation of those ferine traits which make for damage and desolation, without a corresponding development of the traits which would serve the individual's self-preservation and fullness of life in a ferine environment"。足球培养出"exotic ferocity and cunning"，是早期野蛮气质的复兴，却压制了从社会与经济角度看属于"savage character"救赎特征的那些细节。

### 限制与边界

　　作者明确将讨论限定在经济层面，不否认体育可能有审美、道德或其他价值。该论断对接触性、对抗性运动较强，对非对抗性运动较弱。

### 原文摘录

> "The culture bestowed in football gives a product of exotic ferocity and cunning."
> "It is a rehabilitation of the early barbarian temperament, together with a suppression of those details of temperament, which, as seen from the standpoint of the social and economic exigencies, are the redeeming features of the savage character."
> "The relation of football to physical culture is much the same as that of the bull-fight to agriculture."
