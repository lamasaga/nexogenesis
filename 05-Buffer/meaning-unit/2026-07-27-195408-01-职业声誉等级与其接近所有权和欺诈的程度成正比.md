### 核心表达

　　职业的金钱声誉并不取决于其实际有用性，而取决于其与所有权、金融操作的接近程度，以及其是否带有"有用"的污名。直接所有权和金融管理最有声誉，银行业与法律次之，商业再次，体力劳动最缺乏声誉。

### 依据与细节

　　作者描述了一个声誉等级。"Those which have to do immediately with ownership on a large scale are the most reputable of economic employments proper"。其次是直接服务于所有权与金融的职业，如 banking 与 law。银行业因"carry a suggestion of large ownership"而获得声望。法律职业虽不意味着大所有权，但"no taint of usefulness, for other than the competitive purpose, attaches to the lawyer's trade"，因此等级很高。作者甚至说律师"exclusively occupied with the details of predatory fraud, either in achieving or in checkmating chicanery"，成功被视为"a large endowment of that barbarian astuteness"。商业只有 halfway 体面，除非涉及大量所有权和少量有用性。零售生活必需品的商业降至 handicrafts 和 factory labor 水平。体力劳动或指导机械过程的工作"on a precarious footing as regards respectability"。

### 限制与边界

　　该等级是作者所处时代的观察，未涵盖现代专业阶层（如科学家、工程师、医疗专业人员）的声誉变化。它对"有用"的贬义化处理也带有强烈价值判断。

### 原文摘录

> "Those which have to do immediately with ownership on a large scale are the most reputable of economic employments proper."
> "The lawyer is exclusively occupied with the details of predatory fraud, either in achieving or in checkmating chicanery."
> "Manual labor, or even the work of directing mechanical processes, is of course on a precarious footing as regards respectability."
