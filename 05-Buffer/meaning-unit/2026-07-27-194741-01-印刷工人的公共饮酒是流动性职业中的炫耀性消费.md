### 核心表达

　　城市印刷工人中盛行的公共饮酒、请客与吸烟，常被归因于道德缺陷，但其根本原因是该职业的高流动性：工人不断面对新的、短暂的熟人，必须以即时可见的消费来赢得他们的好评。

### 依据与细节

　　作者以 journeymen printers 为例说明炫耀性消费机制。印刷技能在不同城市之间易于转移（"Skill acquired in any printing-house or any city is easily turned to account in almost any other house or city"），该职业需要较高智力，工人对市场机会敏感，工资也相对较高，因此流动性极强。结果是"a great mobility of the labor employed in printing"。这些工人"constantly thrown in contact with new groups of acquaintances, with whom the relations established are transient or ephemeral, but whose good opinion is valued none the less for the time being"。出于 ostentation 与 good-fellowship 的冲动，他们在 dram-drinking、"treating" 与 public smoking 上花费 freely。一旦这种习惯获得流行，社会规范便将其纳入"accredited standard of decency"，进一步推动攀比。作者强调，这种"dissipation"的最终根源，与法国小农的节俭、美国百万富翁捐建大学医院一样，都是"that same propensity for a manifestation of dominance and pecuniary decency"。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未区分不同印刷工种或不同国家城市的差异。该案例旨在说明机制，而非提供可推广的统计结论。

### 原文摘录

> "Journeymen printers may be named as a class among whom this form of conspicuous consumption has a great vogue, and among whom it carries with it certain well-marked consequences that are often deprecated."
> "These men are constantly thrown in contact with new groups of acquaintances, with whom the relations established are transient or ephemeral, but whose good opinion is valued none the less for the time being."
> "The substantial ground of this high requirement in dissipation is in the last analysis no other than that same propensity for a manifestation of dominance and pecuniary decency which makes the French peasant-proprietor parsimonious and frugal, and induces the American millionaire to found colleges, hospitals and museums."
