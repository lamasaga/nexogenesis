### 核心表达

　　相对稳定且艺术上更成熟的民族服饰，多产生于人口同质、流动性低、以炫耀性闲暇而非商品消费为主要竞争方式的社会。现代城市的快速时尚则因炫耀性商品浪费的强势而缺乏稳定性与美感。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，稳定服饰通常"pretty strictly and narrowly localized"，由较贫困、社会同质稳定、流动性低的人群或阶级发展出来。在这些环境中，"the norm of conspicuous waste asserts itself less imperatively"，金钱竞争采取"conspicuous leisure rather than in conspicuous consumption of goods"的方向。因此，"fashions are least stable and least becoming in those communities where the principle of a conspicuous waste of goods asserts itself most imperatively, as among ourselves"。这揭示了昂贵性与艺术服饰之间的 antagonism：在实践上，炫耀性浪费规范与服装应美观合体的要求不相容。这种张力也解释了时尚为何 restless change——仅靠浪费或仅靠美都无法充分解释。

### 限制与边界

　　原文将民族服饰的浪漫化倾向作为观察基础，未提供跨文化的系统比较。该主张是对现代时尚病征的诊断，而非对所有传统服饰的普遍理论。

### 原文摘录

> "Stable costumes which will bear the test of time and perspective are worked out under circumstances where the norm of conspicuous waste asserts itself less imperatively."
> "Fashions are least stable and least becoming in those communities where the principle of a conspicuous waste of goods asserts itself most imperatively, as among ourselves."
> "In point of practical fact, the norm of conspicuous waste is incompatible with the requirement that dress should be beautiful or becoming."
