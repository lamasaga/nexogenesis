### 核心表达

　　Cammack 虽整体看空市场，但得知 William Rockefeller 在按比例卖出 St. Paul 后，反而买入该股；他的目的不是从该股上涨中获利，而是积累弹药，待 Rockefeller 卖完后用以打压大盘。

### 依据与细节

　　金融记者 Joseph 告诉 Cammack，其可靠的 transfer clerk 朋友说 Rockefeller 正在每涨 3/8 点卖出 1500 股 St. Paul。Joseph 预期 Cammack 会做空，但 Cammack 却下令"buy fifteen hundred St. Paul every three-eighths up"。Cammack 让 Joseph 密切关注，一旦按比例卖出停止就立即通知。几天后 Joseph 报告已无股票从 Rockefeller 处流出，Cammack 已积累约六万股 St. Paul。他立即下令卖出这六万股并加仓做空，用 St. Paul 作为杠杆打压整体市场。St. Paul 跌至 44，Cammack 大赚。作者指出，Cammack 的习惯性态度使他瞬间看到比单只股票利润更重要的东西：他获得了启动大规模熊市战役的最佳弹药。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未讨论这种策略在现代监管下是否合法；其重点在于战略性反向思考。

### 原文摘录

> “Billy, go over to the board and buy fifteen hundred St. Paul every three-eighths up.”
> He saw instantly what was far more important to him than his profit on that one stock. He saw that he had providentially been offered an opportunity to begin his big bear operations not only at the proper time but with a proper initial push.
> The St. Paul tip made him buy instead of sell because he saw at once that it gave him a vast supply of the best ammunition for his bear campaign.
