### 核心表达

　　现代 leisure class 主要由在金钱竞争中成功者组成，进入该阶级的通道本身就意味着通过了掠夺性测试；这种持续的选择性准入使上层阶级保存并强化了 barbarian temperament。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，"the leisure class of today is recruited from those who have been successful in a pecuniary way, and who, therefore, are presumably endowed with more than an even complement of the predatory traits"。进入 leisure class 的通道是 pecuniary employments，"these employments, by selection and adaptation, act to admit to the upper levels only those lines of descent that are pecuniarily fit to survive under the predatory test"。若在上层出现返祖至非掠夺型人性，通常会被淘汰回较低金钱阶层，因为"In order to hold its place in the class, a stock must have the pecuniary temperament; otherwise its fortune would be dissipated"。新贵（nouveaux arrivés）是经过挑选的群体，"Barring accidents, the nouveaux arrivés are a picked body"。选拔标准随历史变化：早期野蛮阶段看重赤裸裸的勇力（prowess）、宗族性、凶猛、无耻、坚韧；后来 quasi-peaceable 阶段则转向精明、审慎与欺诈；现代和平阶段更看重金钱能力。

### 限制与边界

　　该模型假设金钱成功与掠夺气质之间存在稳定关联，但未考虑继承财富、教育机会、社会网络等结构性因素。它也忽略了上层阶级中继承的财富可能来自非掠夺性来源。

### 原文摘录

> "Entrance into the leisure class lies through the pecuniary employments, and these employments, by selection and adaptation, act to admit to the upper levels only those lines of descent that are pecuniarily fit to survive under the predatory test."
> "In order to hold its place in the class, a stock must have the pecuniary temperament; otherwise its fortune would be dissipated and it would presently lose caste."
> "The ground of selection has changed, until the aptitudes which now qualify for admission to the class are the pecuniary aptitudes only."
