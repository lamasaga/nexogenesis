### 核心表达

　　精明与凶猛这两种野蛮特质对个人在攀比性竞争中极为有利，且具有高审美价值；但它们对共同体的生活过程没有直接用处，其经济功能类似于将集体资源转化为异己增长。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，"The astute man... is of no economic value to the community--unless it be for the purpose of sharp practice in dealings with other communities"。其功能"not a furtherance of the generic life process"，而是"a conversion of the economic substance of the collectivity to a growth alien to the collective life process"。作者用医学比喻：类似"benign tumor"，有越过良性与恶性界限的倾向。凶猛与精明构成"predaceous temper or spiritual attitude"，是"expressions of a narrowly self-regarding habit of mind"。二者"highly serviceable for individual expediency in a life looking to invidious success"，"Both also have a high aesthetic value"，并且都被金钱文化培养。但"both alike are of no use for the purposes of the collective life"。

### 限制与边界

　　作者承认这些特质可能在与其他共同体的敌对关系中有用，但在现代工业共同体的内部生产中无用。该判断完全从经济功能出发，不涉及道德或审美评价。

### 原文摘录

> "The astute man... is of no economic value to the community--unless it be for the purpose of sharp practice in dealings with other communities."
> "At its best, in its direct economic bearing, it is a conversion of the economic substance of the collectivity to a growth alien to the collective life process."
> "Both are fostered by the pecuniary culture. But both alike are of no use for the purposes of the collective life."
