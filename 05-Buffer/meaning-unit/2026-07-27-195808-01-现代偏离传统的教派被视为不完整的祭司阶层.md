### 核心表达

　　现代某些教派的教士在生活上接近世俗人，关心信徒的世俗福祉，这并非祭司理论的矛盾，而是表明这些教派处于从传统祭司方案向现代形态转变的过程中，属于不完整或混合型的祭司阶层。

### 依据与细节

　　作者预料到批评：许多现代教士明显偏离了上述代理性闲暇/消费的严格方案。他回应说，"we have here to do not with a discrepancy in the theory of sacerdotal life, but with an imperfect conformity to the scheme on the part of this body of clergy"。这些教士是"a partial and imperfect representative of the priesthood"，属于"half-caste priesthood, or a priesthood in process of becoming or of reconstitution"。之所以会这样，是因为这些教派组织的目的中混入了"other factors than those of animism and status"，如社会服务、教育、道德改革等现代功能。作者暗示，随着教派偏离传统信条与仪式越远，其教士生活方式也越不像经典祭司。

### 限制与边界

　　该解释将现代宗教多样性视为传统方案的瓦解或转型，未正面评估这些变化的社会功能或宗教意义。它预设了经典祭司方案为规范性基准。

### 原文摘录

> "We have here to do not with a discrepancy in the theory of sacerdotal life, but with an imperfect conformity to the scheme on the part of this body of clergy."
> "The clergy of the sects and denominations might be characterized as a half-caste priesthood, or a priesthood in process of becoming or of reconstitution."
> "Such a priesthood may be expected to show the characteristics of the sacerdotal office only as blended and obscured with alien motives and traditions."
