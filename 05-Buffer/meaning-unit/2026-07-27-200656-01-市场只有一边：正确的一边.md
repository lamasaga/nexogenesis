### 核心表达

　　股票市场不是多头一边或空头一边，而是正确的一边。投机者必须学会在市场方向与自己判断一致时才进场；若无法确定自己正确，就不应移动。

### 依据与细节

　　作者说，这个一般原则在他脑中牢固确立的时间，比掌握投机技术细节还长。他强调："I must back my opinions with my money. My losses have taught me that I must not begin to advance until I am sure I shall not have to retreat. But if I cannot advance I do not move at all." 他承认人应限制亏损，但这不应导致优柔寡断。他一生犯了无数错误，但每次亏钱都积累了宝贵的"don'ts"，并相信自己还会有下一次机会且不会重复同样错误。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未说明如何判断"正确"的客观标准；"正确"由事后是否盈利定义（"I am only right when I make money"），具有循环性。

### 原文摘录

> They say there are two sides to everything. But there is only one side to the stock market; and it is not the bull side or the bear side, but the right side.
> I must back my opinions with my money. My losses have taught me that I must not begin to advance until I am sure I shall not have to retreat. But if I cannot advance I do not move at all.
> I always knew I would have another chance and that I would not make the same mistake a second time. I believed in myself.
