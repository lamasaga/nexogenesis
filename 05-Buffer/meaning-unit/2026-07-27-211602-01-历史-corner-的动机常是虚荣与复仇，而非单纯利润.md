### 核心表达

　　19 世纪许多大操盘手热衷制造 corner，并非全为金钱，更多出于虚荣心、江湖地位和对同行的复仇；这种非经济动机使 corner 成为高风险、 often unprofitable 的游戏。

### 依据与细节

　　作者列举：Commodore Vanderbilt 的 Harlem corner 赚了，但 Jay Gould 在 Northwestern corner 亏了；Deacon S.V. White 在 Lackawanna corner 赚百万，Jim Keene 在 Hannibal & St. Joe 亏百万。一位老经纪人告诉他，60-70 年代大操盘手的共同抱负就是“work a corner”，有时是 vanity，有时是 revenge。被公认为成功 corner 某人意味着智力、胆识和财力的认可。

### 限制与边界

　　这是口述历史式的轶事，缺乏财务数据验证；作者的道德评判（vanity complex）可能简化了复杂动机。

### 原文摘录

> all the big operators of the ’60’s and ’70’s had one ambition, and that was to work a corner.
> In many cases this was the offspring of vanity; in others, of the desire for revenge.
> It was more than the prospective money profit that prompted the engineers of corners to do their damnedest. It was the vanity complex asserting itself among cold-blooded operators.
