工资差异另一来源是 discrimination：因 race、ethnicity、gender、age、religion、sexual orientation 等 personal characteristics 向相似个体提供或拒绝不同机会。职场歧视反映更广泛社会偏见；经济学家研究以把握 magnitude 与 sources。20-1 节在供求框架内解释多数收入差；20-2 起转向歧视机制——与 compensating differential、human capital、superstar、monopsony、above-equilibrium 并列，构成完整 earnings 解释地图。

> Discrimination occurs when people are offered or denied opportunities based on race, ethnicity, gender, age, religion, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.
