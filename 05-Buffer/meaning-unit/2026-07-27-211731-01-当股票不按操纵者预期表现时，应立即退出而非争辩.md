### 核心表达

　　无论是普通交易者还是职业操纵者，当股票不按照你希望的方向表现时，最明智的做法是立刻退出，不要与 tape 争辩，也不要试图把利润“骗回来”。

### 依据与细节

　　作者强调自己作为操纵者时从未忘记自己也是股票交易者。如果买入不能让股票上涨，他就停止买入并开始卖出；如果股票“waterlogged”涨不动，那就是卖出的时机。他说：“When the stock you are manipulating doesn’t act as it should, quit. Don’t argue with the tape. Do not seek to lure the profit back. Quit while the quitting is good--and cheap.”

### 限制与边界

　　这需要区分“正常调整”与“操纵失败”，判断标准未量化；退出过早可能错过后续成功，退出过晚则损失扩大。

### 原文摘录

> I repeat that at no time during the manipulation do I forget to be a stock trader.
> When the stock you are manipulating doesn’t act as it should, quit. Don’t argue with the tape. Do not seek to lure the profit back. Quit while the quitting is good--and cheap.
> As long as a stock that I am manipulating goes up on my buying I know I am hunky, and if need be I buy it with confidence and use my own money without fear.
