### 核心表达

　　神职人员的举止、服饰和仪式行为模仿世俗仆役的代理闲暇：冷漠、悠闲、程式化、不显露效率，以此彰显其侍奉的神明高于实际需要有用服务的程度。

### 依据与细节

　　作者将神职人员与 footman（男仆）类比。"Priestly demeanor at its best is aloof, leisurely, perfunctory, and uncontaminated with suggestions of sensuous pleasure"。在所有拟人化宗教中，神职生活都带有 vicarious consumption of time 的痕迹。仪式倾向于退化为"rehearsal of formulas"，而"perfunctoriness of the rehearsal is very pleasing to the correct devout taste"，因为它表明神明被抬高到不需要真正有效服务的地位。"They are unprofitable servants, and there is an honorific implication for their master in their remaining unprofitable"。祭司服饰也 notorious 地昂贵、华丽且不便，在不担任神之配偶角色的教派中更是苦行式地不舒适。

### 限制与边界

　　该模型适用于具有等级化神职人员和繁复仪式的宗教。对于无祭司制度或强烈反仪式传统（如某些新教派别）的宗教，其解释力可能较弱。

### 原文摘录

> "It is needless to point out the close analogy at this point between the priestly office and the office of the footman."
> "They are unprofitable servants, and there is an honorific implication for their master in their remaining unprofitable."
> "There should be no show of agility or of dexterous manipulation in the execution of the priestly office, such as might suggest a capacity for turning off the work."
