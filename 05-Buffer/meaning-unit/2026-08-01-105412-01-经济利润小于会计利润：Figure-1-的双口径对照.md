**Economic profit** = total revenue − 全部 opportunity costs（explicit + implicit）；**accounting profit** = total revenue − explicit costs only。Figure 1 图示：会计师路径从 Revenue 减去 Explicit costs 得 Accounting profit；经济学家路径另扣 Implicit costs（total opportunity costs），故 **economic profit < accounting profit**。从经济学家视角，企业须 total revenue 超过 **所有** 机会成本才算真正盈利——仅覆盖付现成本仍可能掩盖隐性资源耗损。

> Because economists include all opportunity costs when analyzing a firm, while accountants measure only explicit costs, economic profit is smaller than accounting profit.
