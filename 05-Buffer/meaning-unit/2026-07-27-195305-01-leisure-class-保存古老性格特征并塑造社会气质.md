### 核心表达

　　leisure class 不仅保守社会制度，还通过教育适应与选择性淘汰保存古老性格特征，导致社会整体气质呈现"精神发展停滞"的倾向。

### 依据与细节

　　第9章开头指出，任何被接受为生活权威标准的倾向或观点，都会反过来塑造社会成员的性格。这种作用一部分通过"coercive, educational adaptation of the habits of all individuals"实现，一部分通过"selective elimination of the unfit individuals and lines of descent"。不适应 accepted scheme 的人或血统会遭受消除或压抑。炫耀性浪费与工业豁免两大原则已被提升为生活规范，成为塑造个体的强制性因素。作者总结："The proximate tendency of the institution of a leisure class in shaping human character runs in the direction of spiritual survival and reversion. Its effect upon the temper of a community is of the nature of an arrested spiritual development." 在社会演化后期，该制度总体上具有保守趋势。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未具体说明哪些性格特征被保存、哪些被淘汰，也未提供实证数据。该主张是演化社会学框架下的一般性论断。

### 原文摘录

> "So soon as a given proclivity or a given point of view has won acceptance as an authoritative standard or norm of life it will react upon the character of the members of the society which has accepted it as a norm."
> "The proximate tendency of the institution of a leisure class in shaping human character runs in the direction of spiritual survival and reversion."
> "Its effect upon the temper of a community is of the nature of an arrested spiritual development."
