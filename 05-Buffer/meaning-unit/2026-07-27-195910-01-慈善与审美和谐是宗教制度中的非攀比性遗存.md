### 核心表达

　　随着经济压力与 status 制度的瓦解，拟人化宗教制度中逐渐混入慈善、社交、审美和谐等非攀比性动机；这些外来因素既帮助宗教制度在形式上存活，又在一定程度上削弱其自我中心的偏见。

### 依据与细节

　　第13章指出，"the anthropomorphic cult... suffers a progressive disintegration through the stress of economic exigencies and the decay of the system of status"。在此过程中，"certain other motives and impulses"与虔诚态度混合，包括"the motives of charity and of social good-fellowship, or conviviality"，即"the various expressions of the sense of human solidarity and sympathy"。这些外部用途"contribute materially to its survival in name and form even among people who may be ready to give up the substance of it"。更普遍的外来因素是"non-reverent sense of aesthetic congruity with the environment"，即在剔除拟人化内容后，崇拜行为留下的审美和谐感。这种审美冲动虽非主要经济性质，但有助于塑造经济目的所需的心智习惯，"mitigating the somewhat pronounced self-regarding bias"从 status 制度传承而来。它与虔诚态度的经济作用方向相反：后者强化自我与非我的对立，前者则通过消除这种对立来弱化自我中心偏见。

### 限制与边界

　　该主张较为纲领性，未详细说明慈善与审美因素如何在具体教派中运作。它为宗教的现代转型提供了一种功能主义解释。

### 原文摘录

> "Among these alien motives which affect the devout scheme in its later growth, may be mentioned the motives of charity and of social good-fellowship, or conviviality."
> "This sense of impulse of aesthetic congruity... has a considerable indirect effect in shaping the habit of mind of the individual for economic purposes."
> "The economic bearing of this impulse is therefore seen to transverse that of the devout attitude."
