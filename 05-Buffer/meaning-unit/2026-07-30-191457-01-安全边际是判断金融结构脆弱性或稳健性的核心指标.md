### 核心表达

　　凯恩斯将经济描述为基于**安全边际**（margins of safety）的借贷体系。安全边际的大小决定金融结构是**脆弱**（fragile）还是**稳健**（robust），并反映经济单位在现金收入短缺时吸收冲击、避免触发**债务紧缩**（debt deflation）的能力。

### 依据与细节

- **三个可识别的安全边际**：
  1. 负债偿付承诺相对于现金收入（payment commitments on liabilities relative to cash receipts）；
  2. 净值或权益相对于负债（net worth or equity relative to indebtedness），即股票市场购买的保证金；
  3. 负债相对于现金及流动资产（liabilities relative to cash and liquid assets），即偿付承诺相对于运营冗余资产的比率。
- 当这些边际变窄时，金融体系从稳健转向脆弱；反之则更有能力吸收现金收入下降而不触发债务紧缩。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提及：未给出各安全边际的具体阈值或临界值；未讨论三个边际之间的权重或相互替代关系；未说明如何动态更新这些指标以进行实时监测；未将这些指标与明斯基的**对冲性/投机性/庞氏融资**分类直接对应。

### 原文摘录

> Keynes identified our economy as being characterized by a system of borrowing and lending based upon margins of safety.

> The margins of safety can be identified by the payment commitments on liabilities relative to cash receipts, the net worth or equity relative to indebtedness (the margin of stock market purchases), and the ratio of liabilities to cash and liquid assets, that is, the ratio of payment commitments to assets that are superfluous to operations.

> The size of the margins of safety determines whether a financial structure is fragile or robust and in turn reflects the ability of units to absorb shortfalls of cash receipts without triggering a debt deflation.
