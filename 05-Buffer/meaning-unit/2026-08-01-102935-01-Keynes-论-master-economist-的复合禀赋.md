Keynes 描绘 master-economist 须 **in some degree** 兼 mathematician、historian、statesman、philosopher：understand symbols yet speak in words；contemplate the particular in terms of the general，在同一 flight of thought 中 touch abstract and concrete；study the present in the light of the past for the purposes of the future；no part of man's nature or institutions lie entirely outside regard；须 **purposeful and disinterested in a simultaneous mood**——如 artist 般 aloof and incorruptible，有时又如 politician 般 near the earth。这是 **tall order**，但 with practice 可 increasingly accustomed to **thinking like an economist**。

> He must be purposeful and disinterested in a simultaneous mood; as aloof and incorruptible as an artist, yet sometimes as near the earth as a politician.
