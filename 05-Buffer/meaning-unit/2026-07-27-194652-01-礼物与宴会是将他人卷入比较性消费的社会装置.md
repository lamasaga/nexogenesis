### 核心表达

　　当个人独自消费不足以充分展示财富时，闲暇阶级借助昂贵礼物、宴会与娱乐，将竞争者与朋友同时变为见证人与代理消费者，从而放大其财富声誉。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，炫耀性消费贵重商品是绅士获得声誉的手段，但"his own unaided effort will not avail to sufficiently put his opulence in evidence"，因此需要借助朋友与竞争者。通过赠送贵重礼物、举办昂贵宴会与娱乐，主人实现双重效果：宾客"consumes vicariously for his host"，同时成为主人无法独自消耗的多余财富的见证人，并目睹主人在礼仪上的娴熟。potlatch 与 ball 等宴会被视为特别适合这一目的的制度。作者承认，这些习俗最初可能源于宗教、社交或 conviviality 动机，但"they required their utility for this purpose very early, and they have retained that character to the present"。因此，即便存在可承认的非攀比动机，其经济效果在 vicarious consumption of goods 与展示昂贵礼仪成就方面并未减弱。

### 限制与边界

　　原文讨论的是"as wealth accumulates"之后的闲暇阶级内部分化，不适用于所有收入水平。作者未量化礼物/宴会支出在个人总消费中的比例，也未讨论现代广告、社交媒体等新型比较机制。

### 原文摘录

> "As wealth accumulates on his hands, his own unaided effort will not avail to sufficiently put his opulence in evidence by this method. The aid of friends and competitors is therefore brought in by resorting to the giving of valuable presents and expensive feasts and entertainments."
> "The competitor with whom the entertainer wishes to institute a comparison is, by this method, made to serve as a means to the end."
> "The latter-day leisure-class festivities and entertainments may continue in some slight degree to serve the religious need and in a higher degree the needs of recreation and conviviality, but they also serve an invidious purpose."
