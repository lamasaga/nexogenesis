### 核心表达

　　决定商品交换价值的劳动量，不仅包括直接用于制造该商品的劳动，还包括用于生产其工具、机器、运输手段和原材料的全部间接劳动。

### 依据与细节

　　Ricardo 以长袜（stockings）为例说明：其价值取决于种植棉花、运输棉花、纺纱织布、建造厂房机器、零售商分销等所有劳动的总和。如果其中任一环节（如航运、建筑、机械）的劳动被节约，长袜就会跌价，因为它能交换到的其他商品量减少。即便是早期猎人社群中，猎杀海狸所需的武器本身也是劳动的产物，因此海狸与鹿的交换比例仍由各自所需的全部劳动（包括制造武器）决定。

### 限制与边界

　　这是理想化的劳动加总模型，现实中难以精确计算所有间接劳动；Ricardo 用它说明原理，而非作为实际核算方法。

### 原文摘录

> the exchangeable value of the commodities produced would be in proportion to the labour bestowed on their production; not on their immediate production only, but on all those implements or machines required to give effect to the particular labour to which they were applied.
> First, there is the labour necessary to cultivate the land on which the raw cotton is grown; secondly, the labour of conveying the cotton... thirdly, the labour of the spinner and weaver; fourthly, a portion of the labour of the engineer, smith, and carpenter...
> Economy in the use of labour never fails to reduce the relative value of a commodity, whether the saving be in the labour necessary to the manufacture of the commodity itself, or in that necessary to the formation of the capital.
