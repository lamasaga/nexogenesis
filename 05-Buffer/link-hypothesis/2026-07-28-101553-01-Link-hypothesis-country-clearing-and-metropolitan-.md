### 核心表达

　　The creation of the country clearing (within the London Bankers' Clearing House) and later the Metropolitan clearing may have lowered the operational barriers to maintaining far-flung branch networks, thereby reinforcing the amalgamation trend that shifted control of provincial clearing houses to London head offices.

### 依据与细节

　　- The country clearing made cheques usable almost universally in England, which would have increased the value of a geographically dispersed deposit base and branch network.
　　- The Metropolitan clearing explicitly collected branch cheques through the clearing house, replacing post and walk clerks, and was so expeditious that even distant London branches received cheques earlier than under the old system.
　　- The text notes that provincial clearings were "ceasing more and more to be under local control, but are controlled by their London head offices" because of amalgamation.
　　- The author speculates that if amalgamation continues, the committee of London clearing bankers might become an important controlling body.

### 限制与边界

　　- This is a weak, speculative association, not a documented causal claim in the source.
　　- The text does not provide data on branch numbers, amalgamation rates, or changes in local versus London control over time.

### 原文摘录

> In these days, too, when the tendency is strong for amalgamation, the local banks are dwarfed by their gigantic competitors, with their branches in many counties and head offices in London, with the result that London each year controls more of the banking in England and the provincial clearings cease more and more to be under local control, but are controlled by their London head offices.
> This may, if the present tendency of amalgamation continues, result in the committee of London clearing bankers becoming an important controlling body, but that time is not yet at hand.
