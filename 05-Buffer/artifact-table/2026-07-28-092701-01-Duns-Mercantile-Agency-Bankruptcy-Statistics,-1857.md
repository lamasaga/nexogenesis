### 关键观察

　　尽管内战期间标准货币极不稳定、价格剧烈波动，但破产企业数和负债额在1862-1865年间急剧下降，表明大多数企业都在盈利。作者解释：商界意识到通胀源于货币贬值，预期战后金价下跌、物价回落，因此主动收紧信用；当1865年1-7月价格骤降时，由于提前准备，破产数反而低于物价快速上涨的前一年。

### 原文摘录

> "The very great decrease both in the number and the liabilities of firms that failed is the best proof that almost all business enterprises were 'making money.'"
> "Such a sudden and violent drop of prices as occurred between January and July, 1865, would have brought a financial revulsion of a most serious character upon a business community under ordinary circumstances."
