### 关键观察

　　- Total assets grew by roughly 8 per cent between June 1914 and November 1915, but the composition shifted markedly: gold reserves and Dominion notes expanded sharply, while loans and discounts contracted.
　　- Central gold reserves grew from $3.05 million to $15.10 million, reflecting the 1913 Act's new mechanism; bills payable fell from about $20.1 million to $5.1 million.
　　- Note: The official reports omitted cents, so the footing totals do not exactly agree with the summed line items.
