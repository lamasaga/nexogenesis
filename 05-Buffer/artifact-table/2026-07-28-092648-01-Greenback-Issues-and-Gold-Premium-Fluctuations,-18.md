### 关键观察

　　每次新发行都反映在溢价上升上。作者认为，1862-1865年间黄金溢价与相对价格中位数高度一致，说明这些波动主要源于公众对作为标准的政府纸币估值变化，因此暂停硬币兑付和法偿法案应对后续价格剧变负主要责任。

### 原文摘录

> "In February, 1862, Congress provided for the issue of $150,000,000 in United States notes or greenbacks."
> "Each new issue was reflected in a rise in the premium."
> "From 1862 to 1865 the premium on gold and the median of relative prices correspond so well that one cannot resist the conclusion that these changes were mainly due to a common cause."
