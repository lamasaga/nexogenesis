### 关键观察

　　银行购买证券的金额已超过资本三倍以上，主要通过创造存款负债实现。这说明银行的核心业务不是简单投资自有资金，而是通过信用创造（deposit creation）来扩大资产规模。储备（现金项目、铸币、法定货币）是限制进一步扩张的关键约束。

### 原文摘录

> "It appears from the above account that purchases of securities have been made to more than three times the amount of the capital, and that this has been effected chiefly by the creation of liabilities in the form of deposits."
> "This provision of cash ... is called the reserve, and the necessity of maintaining a certain minimum reserve fixes a limit to the ability of the bank to increase its securities."
