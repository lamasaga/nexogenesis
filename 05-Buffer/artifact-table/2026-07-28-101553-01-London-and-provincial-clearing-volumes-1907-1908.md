### 关键观察

　　- The town clearing dominated the London total, accounting for roughly 86 per cent of all clearings in 1910-1914, with the metropolitan and country clearings at about 5.5 and 8.5 per cent respectively.
　　- Manchester and Liverpool together dwarfed the other provincial clearings, yet even Manchester's annual total was only about one-thirtieth of the London town clearing.
　　- The 1907-1908 decline in total clearings suggests significant cyclical sensitivity even in a growing payments infrastructure.
