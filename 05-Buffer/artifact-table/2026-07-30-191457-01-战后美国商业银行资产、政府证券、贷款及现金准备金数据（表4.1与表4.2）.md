**关键观察**：(1) 美国政府证券占银行金融资产比重从1946年的57.0%降至1974年的11.2%，表明国债作为头寸工具的能力相对下降；(2) 机构证券在政府证券中的占比从1.3%升至1974年的37.1%，但这些机构债券市场较薄，不适合头寸操作；(3) 现金与准备金/金融资产从1946年的13.6%降至1984年的2.0%，解释了银行为何必须发展多种准备金节约型负债。

> As the size of position-making activity is related to the total financial assets, the decline in the percentage of government securities to total assets (shown in Table 4.1) from the end of World War II until the mid-1970s indicates that government-security holdings were becoming less capable of handling the position-making activity of the banks.
