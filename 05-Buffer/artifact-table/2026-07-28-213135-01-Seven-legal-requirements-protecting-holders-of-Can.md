### 核心表达

　　Canadian law imposes seven requirements on chartered banks that together protect note holders, even though no government security deposit, cash reserve, legal-tender status, or government guarantee is required.

### 依据与细节

| # | Requirement | Key detail |
|---|-------------|------------|
| 1 | Redemption at designated centres | Notes must be redeemed at head office and at treasury-board centres: Toronto, Montreal, Halifax, Winnipeg, Victoria, St. John, Charlottetown |
| 2 | Circulation redemption fund | Each bank deposits with the Minister of Finance lawful money equal to 5 per cent. of its average circulation |
| 3 | First lien on assets | Bank notes possess first lien upon the assets of a bank |
| 4 | Double liability of shareholders | Stockholders are liable to an assessment equal to the par value of their stock |
| 5 | Monthly returns | A detailed statement of assets and liabilities, signed by three general officers, must be filed by the 15th of each month |
| 6 | Canadian Bankers' Association supervision | The association supervises issue and cancellation of notes and administers affairs of failed banks |
| 7 | Interest on notes of failed banks | Notes of a failed bank draw interest at 5 per cent. from the redemption date fixed by the Minister of Finance |

### 关键观察

　　The author argues that only the first requirement—redemption at multiple centres—is absolutely essential; the others could be changed or abolished without impairing the system, because daily redemption at clearing houses makes notes "as good as gold." The 5 per cent. fund, while prominent in textbooks, had not been used since 1890.

> "Following are the legal requirements, which for twenty years have proved adequate protection for the note holder."
