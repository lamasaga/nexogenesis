### 关键观察

　　These four commodities alone represented hundreds of millions of dollars of annual imports. Prior to the European war, approximately **95 per cent** of the credits covering such importations were passed through London as **sterling credits**—drafts drawn in pounds sterling on London. Dollar-denominated credits on New York were "extremely rare" and issued only in exceptional cases. The table therefore anchors the scale of the subsequent shift toward Dollar Credits documented later in the fragment.
