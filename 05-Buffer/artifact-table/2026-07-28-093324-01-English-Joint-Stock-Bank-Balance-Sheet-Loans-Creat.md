### 关键观察

　　六家银行的实收资本仅1600万英镑，却处理了2.94亿英镑的资产负债表。扣除对客户承兑汇票的对冲项目后，对客户负债（current and deposit accounts）2.49亿英镑中，约有1.805亿英镑来自银行对客户的贷款（bills discounted and advances + loans at call）。即“大部分存款不是现金存入，而是借来的信用”。

### 原文摘录

> "The first feature that strikes the casual observer is the smallness of the paid-up capital of the banks when compared with the vastness of the figures that they handle."
> "And it thus appears that nearly three-quarters of the amount due from the banks to their customers are due from their customers to the banks, having been borrowed from them in one form or another."
> "For every loan makes a deposit, and since our balance-sheet shows 180-1/2 millions of loans, 180-1/2 out of the 249 millions of deposits have been created by loans."
