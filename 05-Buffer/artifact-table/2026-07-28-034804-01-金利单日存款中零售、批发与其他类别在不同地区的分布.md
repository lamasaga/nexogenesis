### 关键观察

　　“其他存款”占比随地区接近金融中心而急剧上升：从阿肯色/路易斯安那的约 43%—49%，到南大西洋州的 52%，再到纽约市的 82%。这说明“其他存款”主要与投机和金融交易相关，而非普通零售或批发商品贸易。

### 原文摘录

> "the proportion of 'all other deposits' increases very rapidly as we get closer to the great centres of speculation, and is lowest in rural regions."
> "Retail deposits of 60 millions make up less than one-eleventh of the total. Retail and wholesale deposits together make up about three-elevenths."
