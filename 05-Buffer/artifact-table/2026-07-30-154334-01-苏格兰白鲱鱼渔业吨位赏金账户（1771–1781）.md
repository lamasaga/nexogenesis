关键观察：十一年间赏金总额达 £165,463 14s 0d；按 sea-sticks 折算后每桶 fully packed 赏金约为 £0 12s 3¾d，若计入foreign salt关税并用于home consumption，则每桶实际溢价可达 £1 3s 9¾d。作者指出，对home consumption的herring所免关税应被视为赏金的一部分。

> "Though the loss of duties upon herrings exported cannot, perhaps, properly be considered as bounty, that upon herrings entered for home consumption certainly may."
