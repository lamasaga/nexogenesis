| Category | % of actual GNP | % of target GNP | % of full-employment GNP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Total outlays | 24.7 | 22.5 | 20.35 |
| National defense | 6.5 | 5.9 | 5.30 |
| Net interest | 2.8 | 2.5 | 1.75 |
| Non-OASDHI | 8.4 | 7.6 | 7.00 |
| Social Security | 5.3 | 4.8 | 4.80 |
| Medicare | 1.6 | 1.5 | 1.50 |

OASDHI = Old Age, Survivors, Disability and Health Insurance。来源：Economic Report of the President, February 1984, Table 1.1, p. 29, plus computations。

关键观察：即使按充分就业 GNP 计算，总支出也需从 24.7% 压缩至约 20.35%，意味着国防、利息和非 OASDHI 支出必须大幅调整。
