### 关键观察

Regional clustering is strong: New England houses typically settle with drafts on Boston or New York (or both); Philadelphia-area houses draft on Philadelphia or New York; houses east of the Mississippi draft mostly on New York or Chicago. The 40/30/25/5 split therefore hides a geographical concentration of correspondent relationships rather than a random distribution of methods.

> "Generally speaking, about 40 per cent. of the clearing houses of the United States use drafts on other cities in paying their balances. About 30 per cent. settle by manager's check, and about 25 per cent. settle by cash alone, the remaining 5 per cent. settling by a combination of two or more of the foregoing methods."
