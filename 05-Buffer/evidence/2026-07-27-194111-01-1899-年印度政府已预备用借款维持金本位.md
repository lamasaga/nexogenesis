### 核心表达

　　1899 年印度政府在致 Secretary of State 的函件中表示，若新货币制度初期遭遇饥荒或其他不利情况、而储备尚未累积充足，为维持本位而借款可能成为“绝对必要”；他们更希望获得借款权力而非立即借款。这显示 Gold-Exchange Standard 从建立之初就承认储备可能不足，借款是应急手段之一。

### 依据与细节

　　- 出处：Government of India despatch of August 24, 1899 (H. of C. 495 of 1913, p. 13)。
　　- 背景：新货币制度（1899 年后）初期，adequate reserve 尚未建立。
　　- 态度：政府称“should have preferred to have been armed against such a contingency ... not by actual borrowing but by obtaining power to borrow”。
　　- Secretary of State 在下议院表示：必要时将 resort to borrowing。

### 限制与边界

　　这是脚注中的历史文件引用；原文未说明该借款权力是否后来被正式授予或是否实际动用。

### 原文摘录

> If India were afflicted with famine or other adverse circumstances in the earlier years of our new currency, and before an adequate reserve had accumulated, circumstances might arise in which borrowing to maintain the standard would become an absolute necessity.
> We should have preferred to have been armed against such a contingency ... not by actual borrowing but by obtaining power to borrow.
