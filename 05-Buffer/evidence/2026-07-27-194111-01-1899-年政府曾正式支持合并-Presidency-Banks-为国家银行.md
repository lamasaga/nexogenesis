### 核心表达

　　1899 年 8 月 24 日印度政府在致 Fowler Committee 报告的复文中明确表示，通过合并与吸收三家 Presidency Banks 来组建国家银行是“desirable”。这一官方立场后来因各种讨论和 1901 年的最终放弃而未能实现，说明国家银行理念并非空穴来风。

### 依据与细节

　　- 出处：Government of India Despatch dealing with the Report of the Fowler Committee (August 24, 1899)。
　　- 相关文件：“Papers relating to the Proposed Establishment of a Central Bank in India (reprinted from the Gazette of India and Supplement, dated the 12th Oct. 1901)”记载了最终导致放弃这些想法的讨论。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未解释 1899 年支持立场为何在 1901 年被放弃；这是政治史细节，需查相关文件。

### 原文摘录

> In their Despatch dealing with the Report of the Fowler Committee (August 24, 1899) the Government of India went so far as to declare that the constitution of a State Bank, by the amalgamation and absorption of the three Presidency Banks, was desirable.
