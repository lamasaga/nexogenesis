### 对立 A

　　Currency Reserve 观：储备的唯一目的是确保政府能随时把公众提交的 notes 和 rupees 兑换成 sterling。其上限由可能被要求兑换的 circulation 量决定。

### 对立 B

　　Banking Reserve 观：储备还要确保印度在国际危机中能履行对外支付义务，即使银行体系自身无力偿付。其上限应由可能出现的不利国际收支余额决定。

### 分歧焦点

　　- 若所有银行与商人自身持有充足的 rupee/notes 储备，则政府只需充当 currency converter。
　　- 若金融危机中印度货币市场整体无法履行国际义务，政府是否应坐视 Presidency Banks 暂停现金支付，还是动用 sterling 储备提供支持？
　　- Currency Reserve 的上限取决于 circulation 中可腾出的部分；Banking Reserve 的上限取决于国际收支逆差的极值。两者所需规模可能差异巨大。
　　- 作者先按 Currency Reserve 假设估算“合理”储备约为 £40,000,000（可兑换约 60 crores rupees，即 total circulation 的 25 percent），然后再讨论 Banking Reserve 问题。

### 原文摘录

> Are they held purely as a currency reserve, or are they to fulfil also the purpose of a banking reserve?
> If all bankers and merchants keep adequate reserves in rupees and notes, then it will be sufficient if the Government are always able to turn these rupees and notes into sterling.
> If, on the other hand, it is intended to act as a banking reserve and to ensure India’s ability to meet her international obligations at all times, then its upper limit is set by the probable maximum amount of the adverse balance which could arise against India for immediate payment.
