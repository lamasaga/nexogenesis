VAT 主要问题：regressive—— barely enough to get by 者无法储蓄，全部收入 spent on essentials 故全被税；richer people taxed on smaller share of income。Brookings William Gale 在 Fiscal Therapy 中论证：VAT 可 paired with progressive policies（如 low-income tax credit）counterbalance。另一潜在优点：tax consumption rather than income，tend to encourage saving and investment。Jayachandran 结语：他人不付 fair share 则你的 burden 更高——这解释 VAT 为何在 progress 上是 well-mannered weapon；美国 IRS 估计 self-employment/farm underreport 超 60%，但多数人有 employer 故个税仍 work；VAT could be appealing option for the United States one day，但 regressive 与政治接受度构成张力。
