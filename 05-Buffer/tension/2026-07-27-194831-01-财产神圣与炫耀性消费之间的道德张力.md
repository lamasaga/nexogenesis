### 核心表达

　　现代道德将财产视为神圣，但炫耀性浪费传统又使通过非法手段获取并体面消费财富的人反而获得某种宽容。这两种规范相互冲突，常使社会对财产犯罪的谴责变得不确定。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，在以私有财产为主导的现代社会，"one of the salient features of the code of morals is the sacredness of property"。然而，这一习惯与"seeking wealth for the sake of the good repute to be gained through its conspicuous consumption"相互 traverse。结果是：大额财产犯罪者往往不会受到小规模盗窃者那样的严惩，"A well-bred expenditure of his booty especially appeals with great effect to persons of a cultivated sense of the proprieties, and goes far to mitigate the sense of moral turpitude"。更令人吃惊的是，若犯罪动机是为了让妻子和孩子过上"decent"生活——即支付得起所需的代理性消费——公众倾向于宽宥，"we are prone to condone such an offense where its aim is the honorific one of enabling the offender's wife to perform for him such an amount of vicarious consumption of time and substance as is demanded by the standard of pecuniary decency"。作者总结，围绕不可侵犯所有权的道德体系本身也是"psychological precipitate of the traditional meritoriousness of wealth"，而财富之所以神圣，主要因为它能换来通过炫耀性消费获得的声誉。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提供司法统计或具体案例来量化这种宽容。该张力描述的是一种社会心理倾向，而非法律实证结论。

### 原文摘录

> "Most offenses against property, especially offenses of an appreciable magnitude, come under this head."
> "The thief or swindler who has gained great wealth by his delinquency has a better chance than the small thief of escaping the rigorous penalty of the law and some good repute accrues to him from his increased wealth."
> "We are prone to condone such an offense where its aim is the honorific one of enabling the offender's wife to perform for him such an amount of vicarious consumption of time and substance as is demanded by the standard of pecuniary decency."
