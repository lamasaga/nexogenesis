本章同时主张 **Big Government** 是防止大萧条的核心稳定器，又指出 1970 年代与 1980 年代的扰人通胀"很大程度上归因于我们所拥有的特定 Big Government 结构"。一方面，政府支出须足够大（约 20% GNP）以产生抵消投资骤降的赤字；另一方面，当前政府支出结构（大量转移支付、军事开支、利息支付）推高至 25% GNP 以上，可能加速通胀。稳定利润所需的赤字规模与抑制通胀所需的盈余/支出结构之间存在张力；作者解决方式是重构支出结构（资源创造型项目替代转移支付、加税以在充分就业时实现盈余），而非放弃 Big Government。

> "Big Government can also be a barrier to inflation. However, the proposition that Big Government is necessary does not imply that government need be as big as our present government..."
> "...the disturbing inflations of the 1970s and 1980s were largely due to the particular Big Government we had and still have."
> "A government whose spending is at least 16 percent and perhaps as high as 20 percent of prosperity GNP is necessary to protect the economy against a catastrophic decline in investment and profits."
