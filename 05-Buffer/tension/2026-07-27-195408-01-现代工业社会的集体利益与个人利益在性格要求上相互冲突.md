### 核心表达

　　现代工业共同体的有效运转要求成员具备诚实、勤奋、和平、善意、非攀比等品质；但在竞争制度下，个人的直接利益却由精明交易、不择手段地管理来最好地服务。两种要求指向相反的性格类型。

### 依据与细节

　　作者指出，现代社区的集体利益中心在于工业效率，"The individual is serviceable for the ends of the community somewhat in proportion to his efficiency in the productive employments"。集体利益最好由"honesty, diligence, peacefulness, good-will, an absence of self-seeking, and an habitual recognition and apprehension of causal sequence"等品质来服务。然而，"the immediate interest of the individual under the competitive regime is best served by shrewd trading and unscrupulous management"。上述服务集体利益的品质对个人反而"disserviceable"，因为它们会分散个人追求金钱利益的精力，并使其通过工业的间接低效渠道而非"sharp practice"来获取利益。在 emulation 制度下，每个人都是对手，"each of whom will best attain his individual and immediate advantage if, through an exceptional exemption from scruple, he is able serenely to overreach and injure his fellows when the chance offers"。

### 限制与边界

　　原文将这种张力视为结构性矛盾，未讨论制度设计（如监管、社会保障、合作社）如何缓解该冲突。该模型假设竞争制度必然鼓励损人利己。

### 原文摘录

> "The complex, comprehensive, essentially peaceable, and highly organized mechanism of the modern industrial community works to the best advantage when these traits... are present in the highest practicable degree."
> "The immediate interest of the individual under the competitive regime is best served by shrewd trading and unscrupulous management."
> "Under the regime of emulation the members of a modern industrial community are rivals, each of whom will best attain his individual and immediate advantage if... he is able serenely to overreach and injure his fellows when the chance offers."
