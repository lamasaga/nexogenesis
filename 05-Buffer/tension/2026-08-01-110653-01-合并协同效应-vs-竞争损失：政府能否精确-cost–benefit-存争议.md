阻止合并或拆分亦有代价：企业合并有时通过 joint production 降 administrative 等成本——**synergies**（近年美国银行、航空 consolidation 为例）。Antitrust 若要 raise social welfare，政府须判断哪些合并 desirable、哪些 not，即比较 synergy 社会收益与竞争减少的社会成本。教材指出：**whether the government can perform the necessary cost–benefit analysis with sufficient accuracy** is open to debate；antitrust 适用常 controversial，even among experts。此张力连接效率增益与执法能力边界——不可假设「凡合并皆恶」或「凡协同皆善」。

> It is open to debate whether the government can perform the necessary cost–benefit analysis with sufficient accuracy.
