### 核心表达

　　文本中存在一个未和解的立场张力：作者一方面声称金本位的普及是基于经验与"自然原因"的理性结果，另一方面使用高度感情化的修辞（如"twin sister"、"corpulent"、"Pandora's box"）将支持银币或复本位的观点描绘为不理性或情感用事，这可能削弱其"客观经验总结"的中立性。

### 依据与细节

　　作者把银本位支持者称为"habitually speak of silver as a twin sister to whom some grievous injustice has been done"，并立刻补以"she is a very corpulent one"。在讨论 1834 年法案时，作者称若当时即将意图写入法律，"would have spared us a Pandora's box, to be opened forty years later"。这些表述将货币制度选择问题道德化、戏剧化，而非仅呈现经济机制。

　　同时，作者又不断诉诸"经验""自然原因""市场比率"等客观语言，如"natural causes operating upon men's minds""driven to the gold standard... by the inconveniencies of silver money"。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提及：作者未说明自己的职业背景（《纽约晚邮报》出版人、记者）可能与金本位立场有关；未引用任何反对者的实质性经济论点（如债务人阶层对通缩的担忧、银矿州利益、国际贸易中新兴国家的外汇需求）。

### 原文摘录

> Some persons habitually speak of silver as a twin sister to whom some grievous injustice has been done. All such must admit that she is a very corpulent one.
> It is a great pity that our ancestors in 1834 did not put their intentions into the form of law at that time. If they had done so they would have spared us a Pandora's box, to be opened forty years later.
> Germany is not answerable to us for her tastes. We cannot call her people to account for liking to have gold in their pockets or sauerkraut on their tables.
