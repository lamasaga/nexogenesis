### 核心表达

　　本书的写作与修订始终服务于美国当代政治争论：反对绿背纸币运动与自由铸银。White 明确希望展示"fiat money"可能导致愚蠢、残忍、错误与毁灭。这种明确的论战目的与其声称的"客观历史记录"之间存在张力。

### 依据与细节

　　作者在引言中说："Various editions of the paper were afterward published, among them, two or three for campaign purposes, in the hope that they might be of use in showing to what folly, cruelty, wrong and ruin the passion for 'fiat money' may lead." 他还将自由铸银称为"at bottom, the idea which led to that fearful wreck of public and private prosperity in France"。

　　John Mackay 前言也明确将法国经验与"price regulation""class dissatisfaction""confiscation of property rights under legal forms"等当代现象联系起来。

### 限制与边界

　　原文未提及：未承认 White 本人的政党背景（共和党、金本位支持者）；未讨论法国大革命的具体语境（战争、反革命、社会革命）与美国 19 世纪货币争论的差异；将"绿背纸币""自由铸银"与"assignats"等同可能忽略了三者的制度差异。

### 原文摘录

> in the hope that they might be of use in showing to what folly, cruelty, wrong and ruin the passion for "fiat money" may lead.
> Other editions were issued at a later period, in view of the principle involved in the proposed unlimited coinage of silver in the United States, which was, at bottom, the idea which led to that fearful wreck of public and private prosperity in France.
